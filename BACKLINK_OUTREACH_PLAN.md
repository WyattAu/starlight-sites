## Tools Needed

- **Google Search Console** – Monitor backlinks, identify opportunities
- **HARO (helpareporter.com)** – Journalist query responses
- **Hunter.io** – 50 free email finds/month
- **LinkedIn** – Find editors, writers, outreach contacts
- **Grammarly** – Ensure professional email tone

### Paid (Optional but Recommended)

### Recommended Starting Stack

## Outreach Guidelines

1. **Personalization is mandatory** – Reference specific content on their site
2. **Value first** – Lead with what you offer, not what you want
3. **Follow up once** – Second email 5-7 days after initial, final follow-up 7 days after that
4. **Track everything** – Log sent date, response, link status in spreadsheet
5. **Quality over quantity** – 10 personalized emails > 100 generic ones
6. **Don't spam** – If no response after 2 follow-ups, move on
7. **Build relationships** – Engage with their content on social before emailing
