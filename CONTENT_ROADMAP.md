## 4. Detailed roadmap to 5/5

### Phase C1: Proof completeness (4→5, 4-6 weeks)

The single largest gap to 5/5. Every "it follows that" and "it can be shown that" needs a full proof or explicit reference.

**Target: 0 hand-waves in university math content.**

| Week | Action | Scope | Verification |
|------|--------|-------|-------------|
| 1 | Audit all university math propositions | 447 files | Create a tracking spreadsheet |
| 2 | Complete proofs for linear algebra propositions | ~50 proofs | Peer review against Axler/Strang |
| 3 | Complete proofs for analysis propositions | ~50 proofs | Peer review against Abbott/Spivak |
| 4 | Complete proofs for algebra propositions | ~40 proofs | Peer review against Artin/Dummit |
| 5 | Complete proofs for physics propositions | ~30 proofs | Peer review against Griffiths/Kleppner |
| 6 | Cross-validate all proofs against reference texts | All | Final review |

**Metrics**: 0 "it follows that" in university math; 0 "it can be shown" in university physics.

### Phase C2: Exercise depth (3→5, 6-8 weeks)

The gap between practice problems (computational) and research-level exercises (proof-based).

**Target: Every university topic has at least 3 proof-based exercises.**

| Week | Action | Scope | Reference |
|------|--------|-------|-----------|
| 1-2 | Design exercise taxonomy (computational, proof-based, research-level) | Framework | CLRS exercise structure |
| 3-4 | Add proof-based exercises to linear algebra | ~30 exercises | Axler/Strang exercises |
| 5-6 | Add proof-based exercises to analysis | ~30 exercises | Abbott/Spivak exercises |
| 7-8 | Add proof-based exercises to algebra + physics | ~30 exercises | Artin/Griffiths exercises |

**Metrics**: 90+ proof-based exercises across university content.

### Phase C3: Visual intuition (3→5, 4-6 weeks)

The gap between static text and visual/geometric understanding.

**Target: Every abstract concept has at least one diagram or visual representation.**

| Week | Action | Scope | Reference |
|------|--------|-------|-----------|
| 1-2 | Add commutative diagrams to linear algebra | ~20 diagrams | Axler, category theory |
| 3-4 | Add geometric interpretations to analysis (ε-δ, convergence) | ~15 diagrams | Abbott, 3B1B |
| 5-6 | Add Cayley tables and group diagrams to algebra | ~15 diagrams | Gallian |
| 7-8 | Add force diagrams and phase portraits to physics | ~15 diagrams | Kleppner, Griffiths |

**Metrics**: Every university math/physics concept has at least one diagram.

### Phase C4: Historical context (2→5, 3-4 weeks)

Why was this concept developed? Who developed it? What problem did it solve?

**Target: Key concepts have 2-3 sentences of historical context.**

| Week | Action | Scope | Reference |
|------|--------|-------|-----------|
| 1 | Add historical context to analysis (Cauchy, Weierstrass, Dedekind) | ~10 topics | Abbott, Tao |
| 2 | Add historical context to algebra (Galois, Noether, Emmy Noether) | ~10 topics | Gallian, Wussing |
| 3 | Add historical context to physics (Newton, Maxwell, Einstein) | ~10 topics | Pais, Kuhn |
| 4 | Add historical context to CS (Turing, von Neumann, Dijkstra) | ~10 topics | Copeland, Dyson |

**Metrics**: 40+ topics with historical context.

### Phase C5: Research connections (1→5, 3-4 weeks)

What are the open problems? Where does this lead?

**Target: Key topics mention at least one open problem or current research direction.**

| Week | Action | Scope | Reference |
|------|--------|-------|-----------|
| 1 | Add research connections to algebra (Langlands, Riemann) | ~10 topics | Current survey papers |
| 2 | Add research connections to analysis (Navier-Stokes, P vs NP) | ~10 topics | Clay Institute |
| 3 | Add research connections to CS (P vs NP, quantum computing) | ~10 topics | Current surveys |
| 4 | Add research connections to physics (dark matter, quantum gravity) | ~10 topics | Current reviews |

**Metrics**: 40+ topics with research connections.

### Phase C6: Feature enhancements (4-6 weeks)

| Week | Action | Impact |

| 1-2 | Add prerequisite trees to all university topics | Navigation |
| 3-4 | Add quick reference cards (PDF generation) | Study aid |
