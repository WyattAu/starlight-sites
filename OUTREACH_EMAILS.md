# Outreach Email Templates

All templates follow the formula: **Personalization → Value → CTA → Follow-up**

---

## Template A: Educational Resource Page Outreach

### Initial Email (Day 1)

**Subject Line Options:**
- Quick question about your [specific page name] resource page
- Resource for your [topic] roundup
- Loved your article on [specific topic] – quick suggestion

**Body:**

```
Hi [First Name],

I came across your [resource page/roundup article] on [topic] – really solid collection, especially the inclusion of [specific tool/resource they mention].

I run Wyatt's Notes (wyattsnotes.wyattau.com), a free study resource that helps students with [specific benefit: note-taking, exam prep, study techniques]. We've been growing steadily and recently hit [social proof: X users, X articles, etc.].

I noticed your list doesn't include tools focused on [specific gap – e.g., "Spaced repetition for STEM subjects" or "collaborative note-taking"]. Our resource specifically addresses this with [1-2 sentence description of unique value].

Would it make sense to include us in your roundup? Happy to provide a brief description that fits your format, or if you prefer, I can send over a quick writeup.

Either way, keep up the great work on [their site name].

Best,
[Your Name]
Wyatt's Notes | wyattsnotes.wyattau.com
```

### Follow-Up #1 (Day 6)

**Subject:** Re: Quick question about your [specific page name] resource page

```
Hi [First Name],

Just circling back on my note from last week about potentially adding Wyatt's Notes to your [resource page]. I know these things can get buried.

If it's not a fit, no worries at all. But if you're open to it, I can send over a description tailored to your page format.

Thanks either way!
[Your Name]
```

### Follow-Up #2 (Day 13)

**Subject:** Last note on this – [resource page topic]

```
Hi [First Name],

Final follow-up on this – I know you're busy.

If adding a free study resource to your page isn't a priority right now, totally understood. But if you ever update it, we'd be a great fit for [specific section].

Thanks for the great content you put out.
[Your Name]
```

**Timing:** Send initial email Tuesday-Thursday, 9-11 AM recipient's timezone

## General Follow-Up Rules

1. **Maximum 2 follow-ups** – After that, mark as "no response" and move on
2. **Space follow-ups 5-7 days apart**
3. **Never guilt-trip** – "I noticed you opened my email" comes off as creepy
4. **Always add value** – Each follow-up should include something new or useful
5. **Track everything** – Log sent date, response status, link status in your spreadsheet
