## Usage Guidelines

### Best Practices
1. **Personalize** - Always customize templates for each recipient
2. **Be concise** - Keep emails short and to the point
3. **Provide value** - Focus on what you can offer, not what you want
4. **Follow up** - Send one follow-up after 5-7 days if no response
5. **Track results** - Monitor which templates work best
