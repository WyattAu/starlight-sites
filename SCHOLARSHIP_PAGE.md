# Wyatt's Notes STEM Scholarship

Wyatt's Notes is proud to offer an annual $500 scholarship to support students pursuing degrees in Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics (STEM) fields.

We believe that access to quality educational resources should never be a barrier to academic success. This scholarship is our way of giving back to the student community that has made Wyatt's Notes possible.

---

## Award Details

| Detail | Information |
|--------|-------------|
| **Award Amount** | $500 (one-time payment) |
| **Number of Awards** | 1 per year |
| **Application Deadline** | March 31, 2026 |
| **Award Date** | May 1, 2026 |
| **Payment Method** | Direct to recipient or institution |

---

## Eligibility Requirements

To be eligible for this scholarship, applicants must:

- Be currently enrolled or accepted at an accredited college or university
- Be pursuing a degree in a STEM field (Science, Technology, Engineering, or Mathematics)
- Have a minimum GPA of 3.0 (on a 4.0 scale)
- Be a U.S. citizen or permanent resident
- Submit all application materials by the deadline

---

## How to Apply

### Step 1: Write Your Essay (500-750 words)

Respond to the following prompt:

> **"How has access to free educational resources shaped your learning experience, and what role do you think open-access tools play in the future of STEM education?"**

We want to hear your genuine perspective. There's no "right" answer — we're looking for thoughtful reflection and clear writing.

### Step 2: Gather Required Documents

- **Proof of enrollment** (student ID, enrollment verification letter, or acceptance letter)
- **Unofficial transcript** showing current GPA
- **Optional:** Letter of recommendation from a professor or mentor (not required, but strengthens your application)

### Step 3: Submit Your Application

Email the following to **scholarship@wyattsnotes.wyattau.com**:

- Your essay (as a PDF or Word document)
- Proof of enrollment
- Unofficial transcript
- Optional recommendation letter

**Subject line:** STEM Scholarship Application – [Your Full Name]

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can international students apply?**
A: This scholarship is currently open to U.S. citizens and permanent residents only. We hope to expand eligibility in the future.

**Q: Can graduate students apply?**
A: Yes. Undergraduate and graduate students in STEM fields are both eligible.

**Q: How is the scholarship paid?**
A: The $500 can be sent directly to you or applied toward tuition at your institution. You choose during the application process.

**Q: Can I apply if I'm a high school senior accepted to college?**
A: Yes. If you've been accepted to an accredited institution and plan to enroll in a STEM program, you're eligible.

**Q: Will I receive feedback on my application?**
A: Due to the volume of applications, we're unable to provide individual feedback. All applicants will be notified of the decision by email.
