## The DR/DA structural issue

Ten exam-board sites (dse, ib, alevel, gcse, ap, highers, leaving-cert, cbse,
gaokao, hsc + sat) serve **the same generated topic pages**. Crawlers see
~10 near-identical copies of every topic; link equity and engagement signals
split across mirror domains; pages cannibalize each other's keywords
(150 CANNIB001 findings; the strongest example: 5 pages on one site targeting
"ap computer science").

Options, in order of aggressiveness:

1. **Differentiation (recommended default).** Every board page already
   carries board-specific framing; deepen it — board-specific worked
   examples, spec-point references (AQA/Edexcel/OCR codes), and board-specific
   practice sets. The dup findings then decay as content diverges.
2. **Hub-and-spoke canonical policy.** Pick one primary board site per topic
   family; mirrors emit `rel=canonical` to the primary. Concentrates all
   signals on one URL per topic — but removes the mirrors from search
   (traffic trade-off; a business decision, not an engineering one).
3. **Estate hub.** Grow wyattau.com into the linking hub (topic directory →
   per-board pages), so external links to any mirror lift the whole cluster.
