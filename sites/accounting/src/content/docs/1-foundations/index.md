## Reading the worked examples

The examples are numbered transaction by transaction, and each entry shows both sides of the
equation moving. The recommendation is to cover the entry and predict it: the subject is learned
by producing entries, not by verifying them.

## The standard

Definitions precede use, every mechanic is demonstrated before it is generalised, and every
worked example balances — not as a flourish but as the subject's own verification. The pages keep
the invariant visible at each step rather than burying it under procedure.
