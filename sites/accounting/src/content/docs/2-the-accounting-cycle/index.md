The cycle is the subject executed end to end: transactions to journal, journal to ledger, ledger
to trial balance, adjustments justified by the accrual principle, adjusted trial balance, and the
four statements built from it — with the interlocks shown (income to retained earnings to balance
sheet, still balancing).

- **Journalising and posting** — the mechanics, worked on a running example company.
- **Adjusting entries** — accruals, deferrals, and depreciation, each justified by the accrual
  principle rather than asserted.
- **Statement construction** — the four statements built from the adjusted trial balance, with
  every number traceable.

## Prerequisites

The [Foundations](/1-foundations/) track: the accounting equation, the entry rule, and the trial
balance. The cycle is those facts executed on a running company, period after period.

## What the track is for

The cycle is where the subject becomes a skill: a running company, a period, and the full
sequence from transaction to statements with nothing hidden. The pages use one example company
throughout, so each step's outputs are the next step's inputs — the way the work is actually
done.

## The running example

One example company runs the whole cycle: the same transactions flow from journal to ledger to
trial balance to adjustments to statements, so every step's outputs are the next step's inputs.
When the statements are built, every number traces back through the chain to a numbered
transaction — the traceability is the standard, and the pages maintain it.

## What the cycle is not

The cycle is not the hard part of accounting. The hard part is judgement: accrual estimates,
useful lives, allowance rates. The cycle exists to make the mechanical layer flawless so that the
judgement layer sits on statements that are internally consistent — and the pages keep the two
layers named separately.
