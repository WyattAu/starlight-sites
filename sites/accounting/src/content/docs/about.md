This site provides free accounting notes built mechanics-first: the structural facts stated
precisely, every mechanic demonstrated on a worked example, and every worked example internally
consistent; the statements balance because the entries were posted correctly, and the pages show
the checking.

## The standard

Definitions precede use. Each new mechanic is shown on a worked example before it is generalised.
Adjusting entries are justified by the accrual principle rather than asserted. Where frameworks
differ (IFRS and US GAAP), the difference is named rather than averaged.

## The discipline framing

Accounting's reputation for tedium comes from being taught procedure-first, with the structure
revealed (if at all) at the end. These pages run the other way: the structure is page one, and
every procedure after it is a consequence. On that ordering the subject is short, and the
shortness is the point.

## Reading order

[Foundations](/1-foundations/) before [The Accounting Cycle](/2-the-accounting-cycle/). The
cycle pages assume the equation, the entry rule, and the trial balance as established in
Foundations, and their worked examples run a full period end to end; so reading them first is
reading the payoff without the setup.

## Frameworks

The pages teach the common core that IFRS and US GAAP share, and name the divergence where the
frameworks differ. Where a rule is convention rather than derivation, the site says so -
accounting standards are institutional facts, and the site's honesty about which facts are
institutional is part of its standard.

## Level

The level is the introductory financial-accounting sequence through the four statements and their
interlocks; the material of a first accounting course, done with the discipline of a first
physics course.

## The writing convention

Each mechanic appears three times: stated as a rule, worked on an example, and used in the next
example's input. The repetition is deliberate; the subject is learned by execution, and the
pages are structured so that execution is always against the stated rule rather than against a
remembered layout. Where students typically err (direction of adjustments, which statements a
closing entry touches), the page names the error at the point it would occur.

## The maintenance model

Content is checked by tooling on every change: depth minimums, frontmatter validity, link
integrity, and table formatting. The worked examples carry the site's standard; every number
traceable, every statement balanced; and the tooling enforces the formatting so the examples
stay readable.

## The network

This site is part of a network: exam-board sites for the school-facing business treatments,
subject sites (mathematics, physics, chemistry, computer science, economics, finance) for the
discipline-facing layer. Accounting's neighbours are economics for the transactions' context and
mathematics for the arithmetic's rigor; the links are maintained by tooling.

## How to report an error

Anything that looks wrong; a misclassified entry, an unbalanced example, a definition used
before it is stated; is worth reporting. The fix lands with the correction recorded, and a page
needing repeated patches gets a rewrite, because repeated patches mean the structure is wrong.

## Corrections

Misclassified entries, unbalanced examples, definitions used before they are stated; anything
found wrong is fixed and the correction recorded. A worked example that teaches a broken
mechanic is the site's worst failure mode, and the corrections policy exists because that
failure mode is only detectable by readers doing the work.

## The self-test convention

Self-test items ask for produced artifacts; the entry, the balance, the statement line; not
recognition of them. Producing is the skill the assessments examine and the job requires, and
producing is what the worked examples model.

## The glossary convention

Terms are defined at first use on every page; the glossary is recall, not introduction. The
definitions are written to be self-sufficient, because an accounting definition that requires a
chapter of context is a definition the reader cannot check at the moment of use, and
check-at-the-moment is this site's whole method.

## The first-page guarantee

Page one of every track states the invariants the whole track depends on. If a later page seems
to contradict one, trust the invariant and reread; and if the reread confirms the contradiction,
that is an error worth reporting under the corrections policy above.

## The corrections standard, restated

Balanced is necessary; correctly classified is sufficient; traceable is the standard. Corrections
hold all three.
