equation, the double-entry rule, the accrual principle; generate everything else. Mastery means
executing the mechanics without error and knowing *why* each step is forced, and this site is
built to make the why visible.

## The standard

Definitions precede use. Each mechanic is demonstrated before it is generalised. Every worked
example balances; not as a flourish but as the subject's own verification; and where frameworks
differ, the difference is named. The [study hub](/hub/), [about page](/about/), and
[glossary](/glossary/) carry the details.
