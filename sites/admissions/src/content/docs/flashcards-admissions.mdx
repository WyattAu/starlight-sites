## Common Mistakes

**Rating every flashcard as "easy" without honest self-assessment:** The spaced repetition system only works if you accurately rate your recall. Marking cards as known when you only partially remember them prevents the system from showing you cards you need to review. Be honest — the goal is learning, not inflating your confidence.

**Skipping the explanation after flipping a flashcard:** Even if you got the answer right, reading the explanation reinforces the reasoning and may reveal nuances you missed. The explanation often contains the "why" behind the answer, which is more valuable than the answer itself for exam preparation.

**Using flashcards as a substitute for active problem-solving:** Flashcards are excellent for definitions and key facts, but admissions tests require applying knowledge to unfamiliar problems. Use flashcards alongside practice problems, not instead of them.
