## Complete A-Level Study Guide

A-Levels are the standard qualifications for university entry in the UK, taken by students aged 16-18. This hub page connects you to all our A-Level study materials across every subject.

### FAQ

**How long should I study for each A-Level?**
Most students spend 5-7 hours per week per subject outside of class time. This includes reading, practice questions, and revision.

**What's the best order to study topics?**
Follow the suggested learning path in each subject hub. Foundation topics build on each other, so don't skip ahead.

**How many practice questions should I do?**
Aim for at least 20-30 practice questions per topic, mixing worked examples with independent practice.
