### Modulus-Argument Form

<PracticeProblem client:only="solid-js" question={"Express z = 1 + √3 i in modulus-argument (r, θ) form.&quot; options={['(2, π/3)', '(2, π/6)', '(√3, π/3)', '(2, 2π/3)']} correctAnswer={0} explanation=&quot;r = √(1² + (√3)²) = √(1 + 3) = 2. θ = arctan(√3/1) = π/3 (in the first quadrant). ### De Moivre\'s Theorem

<PracticeProblem client:only="solid-js" question={"Using De Moivre\'s theorem, find (1 + i)⁶.&quot; options={['−8i', '8i', '8', '−8']} correctAnswer={0} explanation=&quot;r = √2, θ = π/4. By De Moivre: (√2 cis(π/4))⁶ = (√2)⁶ cis(6π/4) = 8 cis(3π/2) = 8(cos(3π/2) + i sin(3π/2)) = 8(0 + i(−1)) = −8i."} difficulty="hard" />

<PracticeProblem client:only="solid-js" question={"Express cos(3θ) in terms of cos(θ) using De Moivre\'s theorem.&quot; options={['4cos³θ − 3cosθ', '3cosθ − 4cos³θ', 'cos³θ − 3cosθ', '3cos³θ − cosθ']} correctAnswer={0} explanation=&quot;By De Moivre: cos(3θ) + i·sin(3θ) = (cosθ + i·sinθ)³.
