## About Wyatt's Notes — AP

Wyatt's Notes provides thorough study materials for the US Advanced Placement (AP) programme. AP courses are college-level classes taken during high school, with standardised exams that can earn university credit and demonstrate academic readiness to admissions committees.

### Why Trust These Resources?

These notes are created by **Wyatt**, an educator and content developer with expertise in AP curriculum frameworks. Content is aligned to the College Board's course descriptions, covering the knowledge and skills tested on each AP exam.

Materials emphasise the analytical reasoning and document-based analysis that AP exams demand. Practice problems mirror the format and difficulty of actual AP questions, building familiarity with the exam's structure and timing requirements.

### What You'll Find

- **Subject notes** for major AP courses including Physics, Calculus, and more
- **Exam format guidance** covering multiple choice, free response, and document-based questions
- **Practice problems** modelled on official AP exam styles
- **Content review summaries** organised by exam topic

### Credentials

Wyatt develops resources across multiple international curricula, providing a comparative perspective that enhances AP material accuracy. Content is refined through analysis of College Board frameworks, released exams, and scoring guidelines.
