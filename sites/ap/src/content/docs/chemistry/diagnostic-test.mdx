## AP Chemistry Diagnostic Test

This diagnostic test covers the full AP Chemistry syllabus. There are 20 questions spanning 7 core
topics. The test uses adaptive difficulty, selecting questions based on your performance to target
areas of weakness.

**Recommended time:** 30 minutes. The test presents up to 15 questions drawn from the pool,
adaptively ordered by your answers.

**Intuition:** Chemistry is the science of matter and its transformations. Understanding electron configurations, bonding, and reaction energetics explains why substances behave the way they do.

**Common Mistakes:** Confusing exothermic and endothermic; mixing up ionic and covalent bonding characteristics; forgetting to balance equations before applying stoichiometry.

---


## How the Diagnostic Scoring Works

After completing the test, you receive a results breakdown:

- **Topic Breakdown** -- each topic shows your score, colour-coded green (strong, 80%+), amber
  (moderate, 50-79%), or red (weak, below 50%).
- **Strengths** -- topics where you scored 80% or above.
- **Needs Improvement** -- topics where you scored below 50%.
- **Recommended Study Topics** -- all topics where you did not score in the strong range.

The adaptive engine targets weak topics first, so your weakest areas are tested more thoroughly. Use
the results to prioritise which topic pages to revise.
