## Intuition

**Computer science is the art of solving problems systematically:** It's not just about coding — it's about breaking complex problems into manageable pieces (decomposition), identifying patterns, abstracting away irrelevant details, and designing step-by-step solutions (algorithms). This systematic thinking applies far beyond programming.

**Why it matters:** Computer science powers every modern technology — from smartphones to social media to self-driving cars. Understanding computational thinking helps you solve problems efficiently, whether you're writing code, analysing data, or designing systems.

**The key insight:** The difference between a good and bad algorithm can be enormous. Searching a sorted list with binary search ($O(\log n)$) is dramatically faster than linear search ($O(n)$) for large datasets. Understanding efficiency is what separates casual programmers from skilled computer scientists.

### Computational Thinking

**Intuition:** Computer science is about solving problems systematically — breaking them down (decomposition), identifying patterns, abstracting away irrelevant details, and designing step-by-step solutions (algorithms).

**Common Mistakes:** Confusing abstraction with decomposition; misunderstanding variable scope; mixing up linear search O(n) and binary search O(log n) complexity.
