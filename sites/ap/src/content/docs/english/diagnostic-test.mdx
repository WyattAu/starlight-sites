---


## Intuition

**AP English Language is about understanding how persuasion works:** Every text — whether an essay, speech, or advertisement — uses rhetorical strategies to influence the reader. The three rhetorical appeals (ethos, pathos, logos) are the building blocks of all persuasion. Understanding how authors use these appeals helps you both analyse others' arguments and construct your own.

**Why it matters:** Rhetorical analysis is essential for evaluating news, marketing, political speeches, and academic arguments. In an era of information overload, the ability to identify persuasive techniques and logical fallacies is a critical survival skill.

**The key insight:** Strong rhetorical analysis always connects technique to effect. It's not enough to identify a metaphor — you must explain what it makes the reader think or feel, and how it advances the author's argument. The question is always "why did the author choose this specific technique here?"

## How the Diagnostic Scoring Works

After completing the test, you receive a results breakdown:

- **Topic Breakdown** -- each topic shows your score, colour-coded green (strong, 80%+), amber
  (moderate, 50-79%), or red (weak, below 50%).
- **Strengths** -- topics where you scored 80% or above.
- **Needs Improvement** -- topics where you scored below 50%.
- **Recommended Study Topics** -- all topics where you did not score in the strong range.

The adaptive engine targets weak topics first, so your weakest areas are tested more thoroughly. Use
the results to prioritise which topic pages to revise.
