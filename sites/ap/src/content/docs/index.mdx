## Common Mistakes

**Treating AP courses as easy alternatives to university courses:** AP exams cover college-level material and require serious preparation. The pass rate for AP Calculus BC is roughly 40% for scores of 5 — nearly 60% of students score 3 or below. Underestimating the difficulty leads to inadequate preparation.

**Focusing on test-taking strategies without understanding concepts:** AP exams increasingly test conceptual understanding and application, not just rote memorisation. Knowing the chain rule formula is insufficient — you must understand when and why to apply it in novel contexts. Conceptual gaps become exposed on the free-response sections.

**Neglecting to practise with official College Board materials:** Third-party practice tests may not match the style, difficulty, or content emphasis of actual AP exams. Always use official released exams and scoring guidelines to calibrate your preparation and understand what graders expect.
