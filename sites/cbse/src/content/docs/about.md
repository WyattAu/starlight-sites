## About Wyatt's Notes — CBSE

Wyatt's Notes provides comprehensive study materials for the Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE). CBSE is India's national education board, conducting the Class 10 (AISSE) and Class 12 (AISSCE) examinations that determine university admissions across India and are accepted by institutions worldwide.

### Why Trust These Resources?

These notes are developed by **Wyatt**, an educator and content creator with thorough knowledge of the CBSE curriculum. Content is aligned to the NCERT textbook framework and the latest CBSE syllabus specifications, ensuring direct relevance to board examinations.

Materials are structured to match CBSE's emphasis on conceptual understanding over rote learning. The notes highlight step-marking approaches, common examiner expectations, and the application-based questions that CBSE increasingly uses.

### What You'll Find

- **Subject notes** for Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, and Computer Science
- **Class 10 and Class 12 content** with board-specific exam focus
- **Worked examples** demonstrating step-by-step marking approaches
- **Previous year paper analysis** with topic frequency data

### Credentials

Wyatt develops resources across multiple qualification systems, enabling a comparative approach that strengthens CBSE content accuracy. Materials are informed by NCERT specifications, CBSE marking schemes, and analysis of examination trends.
