## Common Mistakes

**Ignoring the NCERT textbook in favour of reference books:** The CBSE board exams are based directly on the NCERT syllabus. Many students skip NCERT and study from reference books, missing the exact definitions, examples, and problem patterns that appear in the exam. Always master NCERT first, then supplement with reference materials.

**Memorising derivations without understanding the physics:** CBSE physics requires derivations, but memorising steps without understanding leads to errors when the problem is slightly modified. Understand each step — why does this substitution work? What assumptions are being made? This understanding helps when derivations appear in modified forms.

**Not practising numerical problems enough:** CBSE exams include substantial numerical problem sections. Reading theory and watching videos is not sufficient — you must solve problems with a pen and paper. Time yourself to build speed and accuracy under exam conditions.
