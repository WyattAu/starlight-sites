## Intuition

**CBSE provides a structured foundation for Indian science education:** The curriculum covers fundamental concepts in physics, chemistry, mathematics, and biology that prepare students for competitive exams and higher education.

**Why it matters:** Strong CBSE preparation opens doors to engineering, medical, and research careers in India and abroad.

**The key insight:** CBSE exams reward conceptual understanding — rote memorisation without comprehension leads to difficulty with application-based questions.

## Common Mistakes

**Ignoring the NCERT textbook in favour of reference books:** The CBSE board exams are based directly on the NCERT syllabus. Many students skip NCERT and study from reference books, missing the exact definitions, examples, and problem patterns that appear in the exam. Always master NCERT first, then supplement with reference materials.

**Memorising derivations without understanding the physics:** CBSE physics requires derivations, but memorising steps without understanding leads to errors when the problem is slightly modified. Understand each step — why does this substitution work? What assumptions are being made? This understanding helps when derivations appear in modified forms.

**Not practising numerical problems enough:** CBSE exams include substantial numerical problem sections. Reading theory and watching videos is not sufficient — you must solve problems with a pen and paper. Time yourself to build speed and accuracy under exam conditions.

**Ignoring NCERT exemplar problems:** NCERT exemplar problems are harder than standard textbook questions and appear in board exams as "HOTS" (Higher Order Thinking Skills) questions. Many students skip these, losing easy marks on challenging questions that test deep understanding.

**Not revising formulae and key relationships:** CBSE exams test formulae from across the syllabus in integrated questions. Creating a master formula sheet and testing yourself regularly ensures you can recall the right formula under exam pressure.

## Study Strategy

**Daily practice:** Solve 5-10 numerical problems daily across Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics. This builds problem-solving speed and identifies weak areas early.

**Weekly revision:** Revisit topics covered earlier in the week. Use flashcards to test key definitions and formulae. Spaced repetition prevents forgetting.

**Monthly mock tests:** Take full-length mock tests under timed conditions. This builds exam stamina and reveals time management issues.
