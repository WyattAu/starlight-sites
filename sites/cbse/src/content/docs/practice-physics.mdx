**Q2. In Young's double-slit experiment using light of wavelength $\\lambda = 600$ nm, the fringe width is 2 mm. If the distance between the slits is doubled, the new fringe width will be:**

A. 4 mm
B. 2 mm
C. 0.5 mm
D. 1 mm

**Answer: D**
— Fringe width $\\beta = \\dfrac{\\lambda D}{d}$, where $D$ is the distance to the screen and $d$ is the slit separation. If $d$ is doubled, $\\beta$ is halved. New fringe width $= \\dfrac{2}{2} = 1$ mm.
*Difficulty: medium*


**Q3. A convex lens of focal length 20 cm is placed in contact with a concave lens of focal length 30 cm. What is the power of the combination?**

A. $-1.67$ D
B. $+5.00$ D
C. $+0.60$ D
D. $+1.67$ D

**Answer: D**
— $P_1 = \\dfrac{1}{f_1} = \\dfrac{1}{0.20} = +5$ D (convex). $P_2 = \\dfrac{1}{f_2} = \\dfrac{1}{-0.30} = -3.33$ D (concave). $P_{\\text{net}} = P_1 + P_2 = 5 - 3.33 = +1.67$ D. The equivalent focal length is $f = 1/P = 1/1.67 \\approx 60$ cm (convex).
*Difficulty: medium*


**Q4. In a single-slit diffraction experiment, the width of the central maximum is found to be 4 mm. If the slit width is halved, the width of the central maximum becomes:**

A. 2 mm
B. 16 mm
C. 8 mm
D. 4 mm

**Answer: C**
— The width of the central maximum in single-slit diffraction is $2y = \\dfrac{2\\lambda D}{a}$, where $a$ is the slit width. If $a$ is halved, $2y$ doubles. New width $= 2 \\times 4 = 8$ mm. Narrower slit produces broader diffraction pattern.
*Difficulty: medium*

**Q7. In a p-n junction diode, the potential barrier is approximately 0.3 V for Ge and 0.7 V for Si. If a Si diode is forward biased with a voltage of 0.5 V applied, what is the current through the diode?**

A. Moderately high
B. Very high
C. Cannot be determined
D. Nearly zero

**Answer: D**
— For a forward-biased Si diode, the applied voltage must exceed the knee voltage (barrier potential) of approximately 0.7 V before significant current flows. **Q8. In a transistor, the current gain $\\beta = 99$. If the base current $I_B = 20$ $\\mu$A, the collector current $I_C$ is:**

A. 2.00 mA
B. 0.20 mA
C. 1.98 mA
D. 0.99 mA

**Answer: C**
— $\\beta = \\dfrac{I_C}{I_B}$, so $I_C = \\beta \\cdot I_B = 99 \\times 20 \\times 10^{-6} = 1980 \\times 10^{-6}$ A $= 1.98$ mA. The emitter current $I_E = I_C + I_B = 1.98 + 0.02 = 2.00$ mA.
*Difficulty: medium*


**Q9. In an LED (Light Emitting Diode), the colour of emitted light depends on:**

A. The band gap energy of the semiconductor material
B. The intensity of forward current
C. The applied reverse voltage
D. The physical size of the diode',

**Answer: A**
— The colour of light emitted by an LED is determined by the energy of photons released during electron-hole recombination across the band gap: $E = hc/\\lambda$. A wider band gap produces shorter wavelength (higher energy) light. For example, GaAs ($E_g \\approx 1.43$ eV) emits infrared; GaAsP ($\\approx 1.9$ eV) emits red; GaN ($\\approx 3.4$ eV) emits blue/violet.

**Q5. Which phenomenon demonstrates the transverse nature of light?**

A. Interference
B. Diffraction
C. Polarisation
D. Refraction

**Answer: C**
— Only transverse waves can be polarised, since polarisation restricts oscillation to a plane perpendicular to propagation. Interference and diffraction occur for both longitudinal and transverse waves.
*Difficulty: medium*

**Q6. A wire of resistance 12 ohm is cut into three equal parts and the parts are connected in parallel. The equivalent resistance is:**

A. 4 ohm
B. 36 ohm
C. 1.33 ohm
D. 12 ohm

**Answer: C**
— Each part has resistance 12/3 = 4 ohm. Three equal resistors R in parallel give R/3, so 4/3 = 1.33 ohm.
