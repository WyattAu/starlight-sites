## About Wyatt's Notes — Chemistry

Wyatt's Notes delivers thorough study materials for university-level chemistry. These notes cover physical chemistry, organic chemistry, inorganic chemistry, and analytical chemistry, providing the conceptual depth and problem-solving skills essential for chemistry students.

### Why Trust These Resources?

These notes are created by **Wyatt**, an educator and content developer with knowledge of chemistry pedagogy. Content follows the standard undergraduate chemistry curriculum, building from atomic structure through advanced reaction mechanisms.

Materials emphasise the connection between molecular-level understanding and macroscopic observations. Every concept is linked to practical applications, laboratory techniques, and real-world chemical systems, reflecting the applied nature of chemistry.

### What You'll Find

- **Physical chemistry** covering thermodynamics, kinetics, and quantum chemistry
- **Organic chemistry** with reaction mechanisms, synthesis, and stereochemistry
- **Inorganic chemistry** including coordination compounds and solid-state chemistry
- **Analytical chemistry** covering spectroscopy and chromatography

### Credentials

Wyatt develops resources across chemistry, physics, and biology, enabling interdisciplinary connections that deepen chemical understanding. Materials are informed by standard university curricula, research-level insights, and laboratory practice.
