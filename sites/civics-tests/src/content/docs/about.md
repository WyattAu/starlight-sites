## About Wyatt's Notes — Civics Tests

Wyatt's Notes delivers comprehensive study materials for US citizenship and civics tests. These notes cover the USCIS naturalisation test, civics questions, and the English language requirements, building the knowledge needed to become a US citizen.

### Why Trust These Resources?

These notes are developed by **Wyatt**, an educator and content creator with knowledge of the US naturalisation process. Content is aligned to the current USCIS civics test syllabus, covering all 100 possible civics questions.

Materials emphasise the historical and governmental knowledge that the citizenship test requires. Every topic reflects the actual test format and the civic understanding that informed citizenship demands.

### What You'll Find

- **Civics questions** covering American government, history, and geography
- **English test preparation** for the reading and writing requirements
- **Interview preparation** for the naturalisation interview
- **Study guides** organised for efficient test preparation
- **Practice tests** simulating the actual civics test experience

### Credentials

Wyatt develops resources across standardised testing and qualifications, providing the assessment expertise that enhances civics test materials. Content is informed by USCIS specifications, official practice materials, and analysis of test patterns.
