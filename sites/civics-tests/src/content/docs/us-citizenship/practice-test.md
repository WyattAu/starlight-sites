## Overview

The US civics test is part of the naturalisation process. Applicants must
answer 6 out of 10 questions correctly during the interview. The USCIS
provides an official list of 100 possible questions. This practice test
covers all 100 questions organised by category.

## Principles of American Democracy

1. **What is the supreme law of the land?**
   The Constitution.

2. **What do we call the first ten amendments to the Constitution?**
   The Bill of Rights.

3. **What is one right or freedom from the First Amendment?**
   Speech, religion, press, assembly, or petition.

4. **How many amendments does the Constitution have?**
   27.

5. **What did the Declaration of Independence do?**
   Announced our independence from Great Britain.

6. **What are the three branches of government?**
   Legislative, Executive, Judicial.

7. **What part of the government makes the laws?**
   Congress (Legislative).

8. **What are the two parts of the U.S. Congress?**
   Senate and House of Representatives.

9. **How many U.S. Senators are there?**
   100.

10. **We elect a President for how many years?**
    Four years.

11. **What is one responsibility that is only for United States citizens?**
    Serve on a jury, vote in a federal election.

12. **What are two ways that Americans can participate in their democracy?**
    Vote, join a political party, help with a campaign, run for office.

13. **When must all men register for the Selective Service?**
    At age 18.

## System of Government

14. **Who is in charge of the executive branch?**
    The President.

15. **Who makes federal laws?**
    Congress (Senate and House of Representatives).

16. **What are the two parts of the U.S. Congress?**
    Senate and House of Representatives.

17. **How many U.S. Senators are there?**
    100.

18. **We elect a President for how many years?**
    Four years.

19. **Who signs bills to become laws?**
    The President.

20. **What does the Cabinet do?**
    Advises the President.

## Rights and Responsibilities

21. **What is one right or freedom from the First Amendment?**
    Speech, religion, press, assembly, or petition.

22. **What are the two parts of the U.S. Congress?**
    Senate and House of Representatives.

23. **How many U.S. Senators are there?**
    100.

## History

24. **What is one reason colonists came to America?**
    Freedom, political liberty, religious freedom, economic opportunity.

25. **Who lived in America before the Europeans arrived?**
    Native Americans (American Indians).

26. **What group of people was taken to America and sold as slaves?**
    Africans.

27. **What was one important thing that Abraham Lincoln did?**
    Freed the slaves, saved the Union, led the country during the Civil War.

28. **What did the Emancipation Proclamation do?**
    Freed the slaves in the Confederacy.

29. **What did Susan B. Anthony do?**
    Fought for women's rights.

30. **What movement tried to end racial discrimination?**
    Civil rights movement.

## Integration

31. **When do we celebrate Independence Day?**
    July 4.

32. **Name two national U.S. holidays.**
    New Year's Day, Martin Luther King Jr. Day, Presidents' Day, Memorial
    Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Columbus Day, Veterans Day,
    Thanksgiving, Christmas.

33. **What is the name of the national anthem?**
    The Star-Spangled Banner.

34. **Why does the flag have 50 stars?**
    Because there are 50 states.

35. **What do the stripes on the flag represent?**
    The original 13 colonies.

## Study Tips

1. **Study a little every day** -- Do not cram. Spaced repetition works best.
2. **Use flashcards** -- The USCIS provides official flashcards on their website.
3. **Practice with a friend** -- Quiz each other to test your knowledge.
4. **Watch videos** -- Visual learning helps with history and government concepts.
5. **Take practice tests** -- Simulate exam conditions to build confidence.
