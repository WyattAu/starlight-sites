## Common Mistakes

**Confusing decidable with decidable in polynomial time:** Decidable means a Turing machine can solve it (halts on all inputs). Polynomial time means it can be solved efficiently. Some problems are decidable but require exponential time.

**Forgetting that NP includes P:** P ⊆ NP. All problems solvable in polynomial time are also verifiable in polynomial time. Don't assume NP problems are always hard.

**Mixing up reduction with simulation:** Reduction transforms one problem into another. Simulation runs one machine on another's input. They're different concepts in computability theory.
