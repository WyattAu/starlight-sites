## About Wyatt's Notes — Dart

Wyatt's Notes provides thorough study materials for Dart programming and Flutter development. These notes cover Dart's syntax, async patterns, widget system, and cross-platform mobile development, building the skills needed for production Flutter applications.

### Why Trust These Resources?

These notes are developed by **Wyatt**, an educator and content developer with expertise in Dart and Flutter ecosystems. Content follows a structured progression from language fundamentals through Flutter's widget architecture to practical app development.

Materials emphasise Dart's async/await model, null safety, and the widget composition pattern that defines Flutter development. Every concept includes practical examples and explains the cross-platform considerations that matter in production apps.

### What You'll Find

- **Language fundamentals** covering types, functions, and control flow
- **Async programming** with Futures, streams, and async/await
- **Null safety** with sound null safety and late variables
- **Flutter basics** covering widgets, state management, and layouts
- **Platform integration** for iOS, Android, web, and desktop

### Credentials

Wyatt develops resources across Dart, mobile development, and programming, providing the cross-platform perspective that enhances Dart materials. Content is informed by Dart specification, Flutter documentation, and production app analysis.
