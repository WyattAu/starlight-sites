## About Wyatt's Notes — EU Driving

Wyatt's Notes offers comprehensive study materials for EU driving licence tests. These notes cover the European driving regulations, road signs, theory test preparation, and the driving skills required for EU licence categories.

### Why Trust These Resources?

These notes are developed by **Wyatt**, an educator and content developer with knowledge of driving regulations across EU member states. Content covers the common EU driving theory framework while addressing national variations.

Materials emphasise the road safety knowledge and practical driving skills that responsible EU drivers need. Every topic reflects the actual test format and the real-world driving scenarios that new licence holders encounter.

### What You'll Find

- **EU driving regulations** covering speed limits, right-of-way, and road rules
- **European road signs** with identification and meaning for all sign categories
- **Theory test preparation** with questions modelled on EU member state exams
- **Licence categories** covering B, A, C, and D classifications
- **Cross-border driving** rules for driving across EU member states

### Credentials

Wyatt develops resources across driving tests internationally, providing comparative knowledge that strengthens EU driving materials. Content is informed by EU driving regulations, national handbooks, and analysis of test requirements.
