## Overview

The EU driving theory test covers road signs, traffic rules, safe driving,
and EU-specific regulations. While each EU country has its own theory test,
the questions are based on the Vienna Convention standards. This practice
test covers common questions across EU countries.

## Road Signs Questions

### Question 1

**What does a red circular sign with a white horizontal bar indicate?**

a) No parking
b) No entry
c) Speed limit
d) Yield

**Answer**: b) No entry. A red circle with a white horizontal bar is the
Vienna Convention sign for "No entry" -- vehicles are prohibited from entering
the road in that direction.

### Question 2

**What is the speed limit in built-up areas in most EU countries?**

a) 30 km/h
b) 50 km/h
c) 60 km/h
d) 80 km/h

**Answer**: b) 50 km/h. The standard speed limit in built-up areas across
EU countries is 50 km/h (approximately 31 mph), unless otherwise signed.

### Question 3

**What does a blue circular sign with a white arrow indicate?**

a) Warning
b) Information
c) Mandatory direction
d) Prohibition

**Answer**: c) Mandatory direction. Blue circular signs with white arrows
indicate actions that must be taken (mandatory direction).

## Driving Laws Questions

### Question 4

**What is the general alcohol limit for drivers in most EU countries?**

a) 0.00% BAC
b) 0.02% BAC
c) 0.05% BAC
d) 0.08% BAC

**Answer**: c) 0.05% BAC. Most EU countries have a general alcohol limit of
0.05% BAC (50mg/100ml). Some countries (Sweden, Czech Republic) have lower
or zero limits.

### Question 5

**What documents must you carry when driving in the EU?**

a) Driving licence only
b) Driving licence and registration
c) Driving licence, registration, insurance, and ID
d) Driving licence and passport only

**Answer**: c) Driving licence, registration, insurance, and ID. You must
carry a valid driving licence, vehicle registration, proof of insurance, and
passport or national ID card.

### Question 6

**At what age must children use appropriate child restraints in the EU?**

a) Under 10 years
b) Under 12 years or shorter than 150cm
c) Under 15 years
d) All children must use restraints

**Answer**: b) Under 12 years or shorter than 150cm. EU regulations require
appropriate child restraints for children under 12 years or shorter than
150cm.

## Safe Driving Questions

### Question 7

**What should you do when driving in a foreign EU country for the first time?**

a) Drive at the speed limit of your home country
b) Research local traffic laws and road signs before driving
c) Follow other drivers
d) Drive on the right side of the road

**Answer**: b) Research local traffic laws and road signs before driving.
Traffic laws, speed limits, and signs may differ from your home country.

### Question 8

**What is the minimum following distance on motorways in good conditions?**

a) 1 second
b) 2 seconds
c) 3 seconds
d) 5 seconds

**Answer**: b) 2 seconds. The recommended minimum following distance on
motorways in good conditions is 2 seconds. Increase to 4-6 seconds in
adverse conditions.

## Practice Tips

1. **Study the Vienna Convention signs**: These are standardised across the
   EU and form the basis of all EU theory tests.

2. **Know country-specific variations**: While signs are standardised, speed
   limits and rules may vary by country.

3. **Review alcohol limits**: Know the BAC limits for each country you plan
   to visit (0.05% is standard, but Sweden is 0.02%, Czech Republic is 0.00%).

4. **Practice with official materials**: Many EU countries provide official
   practice tests on their government websites.

## Summary

The EU driving theory test covers Vienna Convention road signs (red circles for
regulations, triangles for warnings, rectangles for information), standard
driving laws (50/90/120 km/h speed limits, 0.05% BAC), and safe driving
practices. While the theory test varies by country, the underlying standards
are harmonised through the Vienna Convention. Study country-specific rules
before driving abroad.
