## About Wyatt's Notes — UK Driving

Wyatt's Notes provides thorough study materials for the UK driving theory test. These notes cover the Highway Code, road signs, hazard perception, and theory test preparation, building the knowledge needed to pass the DVSA theory and practical driving tests.

### Why Trust These Resources?

These notes are developed by **Wyatt**, an educator and content creator with knowledge of UK driving regulations and test requirements. Content is aligned to the current DVSA (Driver and Vehicle Standards Agency) theory test syllabus.

Materials emphasise the practical road knowledge and hazard awareness that safe driving requires. Every topic reflects the actual test format and the real-world driving scenarios that the theory test assesses.

### What You'll Find

- **Highway Code** covering rules, regulations, and best practices
- **Road signs** with identification and meaning for all UK sign categories
- **Hazard perception** techniques and video clip analysis
- **Theory test practice** with questions modelled on the official test
- **Mock tests** simulating the actual theory test experience

### Credentials

Wyatt develops resources across driving tests internationally, providing comparative knowledge that strengthens UK driving materials. Content is informed by DVSA specifications, the Highway Code, and analysis of theory test patterns.
