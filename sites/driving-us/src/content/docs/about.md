## About Wyatt's Notes — US Driving

Wyatt's Notes delivers comprehensive study materials for the US driving test. These notes cover state-specific road rules, traffic signs, written test preparation, and driving skills, building the knowledge needed to pass DMV examinations across US states.

### Why Trust These Resources?

These notes are created by **Wyatt**, an educator and content developer with knowledge of US driving regulations across multiple states. Content covers the common elements of DMV tests while highlighting state-specific variations.

Materials emphasise the practical driving knowledge and road safety awareness that responsible driving requires. Every topic reflects the actual test format and real-world driving scenarios that new drivers encounter.

### What You'll Find

- **Road rules** covering speed limits, right-of-way, and traffic laws
- **Traffic signs** with identification and meaning for all US sign categories
- **Written test preparation** with questions modelled on DMV exams
- **Practice tests** simulating the actual written test experience
- **State-specific guides** for major US states

### Credentials

Wyatt develops resources across driving tests internationally, providing comparative knowledge that strengthens US driving materials. Content is informed by state DMV handbooks, federal driving guidelines, and analysis of test patterns.
