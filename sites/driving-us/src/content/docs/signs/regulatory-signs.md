## Overview

Regulatory signs inform road users of traffic laws and regulations. They are
usually white, black, and red. Disobeying a regulatory sign is a traffic
violation. The Manual on Uniform Traffic Control Devices (MUTCD) classifies
these signs into several categories.

## Speed Limit Signs

Speed limit signs are white rectangles with black text. They indicate the
maximum or minimum speed permitted on a road segment.

- **Maximum Speed Limit**: The highest speed allowed under ideal conditions
- **Minimum Speed Limit**: The lowest speed required to maintain traffic flow
- **Speed Zone**: Indicates an area with a reduced speed limit ahead

### Key Rules

- Speed limits are maximums for ideal conditions; you must drive slower in
  rain, fog, heavy traffic, or construction zones.
- School zones have reduced limits during posted hours.
- Work zones have doubled fines for violations.

## Prohibition Signs

Prohibition signs are typically red circles with a white background and a
black symbol crossed out by a red diagonal line.

- **No Entry**: Red circle with white horizontal bar; vehicles prohibited
- **No Right Turn**: Red circle with right arrow crossed out
- **No Left Turn**: Red circle with left arrow crossed out
- **No U-Turn**: Red circle with U-shaped arrow crossed out
- **No Overtaking**: Red circle with two cars side by side crossed out
- **No Parking**: Red circle with blue background and red diagonal line

## Mandatory Signs

Mandatory signs are blue circles with white arrows. They indicate required
actions.

- **Turn Left**: Must turn left at the intersection
- **Turn Right**: Must turn right at the intersection
- **Proceed Straight**: Must continue straight ahead
- **Roundabout**: Must proceed clockwise around the roundabout

## Warning Signs

Warning signs are yellow diamonds with black symbols. They alert drivers to
hazards ahead.

- **Curve Ahead**: Road curves to the right or left
- **Merge**: Traffic merges from the right or left
- **Pedestrian Crossing**: Pedestrians may be crossing
- **School Zone**: Children may be present
- **Road Work Ahead**: Construction or maintenance ahead

## Common Pitfalls

1. **Confusing warning and regulatory signs**: Warning signs are yellow
   diamonds (hazards); regulatory signs are white/red (laws). Disobeying a
   regulatory sign is a violation; ignoring a warning sign is dangerous but
   not necessarily illegal.

2. **Ignoring speed limit changes**: Speed limits change at posted signs,
   school zones, and construction zones. Failing to reduce speed is a
   common cause of tickets.

3. **Misunderstanding right-of-way signs**: Stop signs and yield signs have
   specific rules. A stop sign requires a complete stop; a yield sign allows
   proceeding if the way is clear.

## Summary

Regulatory signs are the legal backbone of traffic control. They use standard
MUTCD colours and shapes: white rectangles for speed limits, red circles for
prohibitions, blue circles for mandatory actions, and yellow diamonds for
warnings. Understanding these signs is essential for passing the written
driving test and for safe driving.

## Worked Examples

### Example 1: Speed Limit Scenario

You are driving on a road posted with a 45 MPH speed limit. It begins to
rain heavily and visibility drops to 200 feet.

**Question**: What is the legal speed?

**Answer**: You must drive at a speed that is "reasonable and prudent" for the
conditions, even if that is below the posted limit. The posted limit is the
maximum for ideal conditions, not a guaranteed safe speed.

### Example 2: Sign Identification

You approach an intersection and see a white octagonal sign with "STOP" in
red letters.

**Identification**: This is a regulatory stop sign. You must come to a
complete stop before the stop line or crosswalk, then proceed only when the
way is clear.
