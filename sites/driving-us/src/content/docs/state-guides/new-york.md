## The Agency: New York DMV

New York State's DMV licenses drivers statewide; New York City offices
are among the busiest in the country, so reserve appointments online.
Note that New York City proper has its own quirks (street cleaning,
alternate-side parking) that appear in the state manual.

## Learner Permit

- **Minimum age**: 16.
- **Knowledge test**: 20 questions; you must answer **14 correctly**,
  including at least **2 of the 4 road-sign questions**. The dual
  threshold trips up more testers than the raw score suggests.
- **Vision test** at the DMV, or submit a vision-test report from a
  licensed provider.
- **Fee**: application fee plus the driver-license fee varies by
  region (NYC costs more than upstate).
- **Supervision**: a licensed driver 21+; in New York City, permit
  holders may only drive with a driving-school instructor in a
  dual-control car — a rule unique among big states.

## Mandatory Courses

Before the road test, every New York driver under 18 must complete:

- **50 hours of supervised practice (15 at night)**, certified by a
  parent or guardian, and
- A **5-hour pre-licensing course** (or a full 48-hour driver
  education course in lieu of it).

## Junior License (16½-17)

Passing the road test yields a **junior license** with restrictions:

- No driving in New York City for junior licensees (any time) —
  upstate junior drivers must plan around this.
- **9 p.m. to 5 a.m.** curfew, with limited exceptions.
- At 17, a junior license upgrades to full after completing a state-
  approved driver education course (otherwise at 18).

## What the NYS Written Test Emphasizes

From the official Driver's Manual:

- Right-of-way: four-way stops, left turns, U-turns, and emergency
  vehicles — New York word-questions these carefully.
- Signs: 4 of 20 questions; missing the 2-of-4 sign threshold fails
  the whole test even with 14 correct elsewhere.
- Alcohol: 0.08% adult limit, **zero tolerance (0.02%) under 21**,
  and the state's steep administrative license suspension.
- School bus rules: stopping distance and the red-light passing ban.
- Parking: uphill/downhill curb positions, fire hydrants, and NYC
  alternate-side rules.

## Study Plan for the New York Permit Test

1. Read the manual's chapters 4 (signs) and 8 (alcohol) twice — they
   gate both thresholds.
2. Practice sign recognition until the 2-of-4 bar is trivially safe.
3. Aim for 19-20/20 on practice tests; the 14/20 margin looks safe
   but the embedded sign requirement changes the math.
4. Book the road test the day you finish the 50 hours — upstate
   openings clear fast but NYC slots take weeks.

## Common New York Test Mistakes

- Missing that permit holders cannot drive on any NYC street except
  with a driving school.
- Confusing zero-tolerance BAC (0.02%) with the adult limit.
- Forgetting U-turns are illegal in business districts in New York.
- Misreading the school-bus rule: on divided highways, traffic
  behind the bus (and oncoming on undivided roads) must stop.
