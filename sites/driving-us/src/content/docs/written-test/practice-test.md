## Overview

The US driving written test covers traffic signs, traffic rules, safe driving
techniques, and vehicle laws. This practice test includes questions similar
to those on the actual test. Most states require a score of 80-90% to pass.

## Traffic Signs Questions

### Question 1

**What does a red octagonal sign indicate?**

a) Yield
b) Stop
c) No entry
d) Speed limit

**Answer**: b) Stop. A red octagonal (eight-sided) sign with "STOP" in white
letters means you must come to a complete stop before the stop line or
crosswalk.

### Question 2

**What does a yellow diamond sign indicate?**

a) Speed limit
b) Warning of a hazard ahead
c) No parking
d) Hospital ahead

**Answer**: b) Warning of a hazard ahead. Yellow diamond signs warn drivers
of upcoming hazards such as curves, merges, or pedestrian crossings.

### Question 3

**What does a white rectangular sign with black text indicate?**

a) Warning
b) Regulation (law)
c) Information
d) Direction

**Answer**: b) Regulation (law). White rectangular signs with black text
indicate traffic laws and regulations such as speed limits.

## Traffic Rules Questions

### Question 4

**At a four-way stop, who goes first when two vehicles arrive simultaneously?**

a) The larger vehicle
b) The vehicle on the right
c) The vehicle going straight
d) The vehicle turning left

**Answer**: b) The vehicle on the right. When two vehicles arrive at a
four-way stop at the same time, the vehicle on the right has the right of way.

### Question 5

**What is the minimum following distance you should maintain behind the
vehicle in front of you?**

a) 1 second
b) 2 seconds
c) 3 seconds
d) 5 seconds

**Answer**: c) 3 seconds. The 3-second rule provides adequate time to react
and stop safely under normal conditions. Increase to 4-6 seconds in adverse
conditions.

### Question 6

**When must you yield to an emergency vehicle?**

a) Only when it is behind you
b) Only at intersections
c) Whenever you hear a siren or see flashing lights
d) Only on highways

**Answer**: c) Whenever you hear a siren or see flashing lights. You must
pull over to the right edge of the road and stop until the emergency vehicle
passes.

## Safe Driving Questions

### Question 7

**What should you do if you feel drowsy while driving?**

a) Turn up the radio
b) Open the window for fresh air
c) Pull over and rest
d) Drink coffee

**Answer**: c) Pull over and rest. Nothing sobers you up except time. Coffee,
fresh air, and turning up the radio do not prevent fatigue-related accidents.

### Question 8

**What is the recommended following distance in rain?**

a) 2 seconds
b) 3 seconds
c) 4-6 seconds
d) 8-10 seconds

**Answer**: c) 4-6 seconds. In rain, increase your following distance to
4-6 seconds to allow more time to react and stop safely.

### Question 9

**What should you do if your brakes fail?**

a) Turn off the engine immediately
b) Pump the brakes rapidly
c) Use the emergency brake and steer to safety
d) All of the above

**Answer**: d) All of the above. Try pumping brakes first, then use the
emergency brake, downshift, and steer to a safe location.

## DUI/DWI Questions

### Question 10

**What is the legal BAC limit for drivers aged 21 and over in the US?**

a) 0.04%
b) 0.06%
c) 0.08%
d) 0.10%

**Answer**: c) 0.08%. All states have adopted 0.08% as the legal BAC limit
for drivers aged 21 and over. Lower limits apply to commercial drivers (0.04%)
and drivers under 21.

### Question 11

**What happens if you refuse a chemical test when arrested for DUI?**

a) Nothing happens
b) Your license is automatically suspended
c) You can only be charged with a misdemeanor
d) The charges are dropped

**Answer**: b) Your license is automatically suspended. All states have
implied consent laws -- by driving on public roads, you consent to chemical
testing when lawfully arrested for DUI.

## Practice Tips

1. **Study the driver handbook**: Each state publishes an official driver
   handbook with all the rules and signs you need to know.

2. **Take practice tests**: Use online practice tests to familiarize yourself
   with the format and types of questions.

3. **Focus on signs**: Traffic signs are a major component of the written
   test. Know the difference between regulatory, warning, and information
   signs.

4. **Review DUI laws**: DUI/DWI questions are common on the written test.
   Know the legal BAC limits and penalties.

5. **Don't rush**: Take your time reading each question carefully. Many
   mistakes are caused by rushing through the test.
