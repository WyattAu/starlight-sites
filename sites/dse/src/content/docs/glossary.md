## General Examination Terms

**Compulsory Module**: A module that all candidates must take as part of a subject. Forms the foundation of the subject curriculum.

**Core Subject**: A subject that all candidates must study. Includes Chinese, English, Mathematics, and Liberal Studies.

**DSE (Diploma of Secondary Education)**: Hong Kong's university entrance examination, taken by secondary school students.

**Elective Subject**: A subject chosen by candidates based on their interests and strengths. Allows specialization.

**Grading System**: DSE results are reported on a 7-level scale: 5**, 5*, 5, 4, 3, 2, 1, and U (ungraded).

**Paper**: A component of a subject's examination, typically covering different skills or content areas.

**Paper 1**: Usually the reading/writing or listening component, depending on the subject.

**Paper 2**: Usually the writing or integrated component, depending on the subject.

**Speaking Assessment**: An oral examination component testing communication skills. May be conducted in groups or individually.

**Subject Benchmark**: The standard required to achieve each grade level. Set by the HKEAA based on candidate performance.

## Chinese Language

**Classical Chinese (文言文)**: Literary Chinese used in classical texts. Requires understanding of classical grammar, vocabulary, and allusions.

**Chinese Literature (中國文學)**: An elective exploring Chinese literary works including poetry, prose, fiction, and drama across different periods.

**Close Reading (閱讀理解)**: A paper testing comprehension of Chinese texts through questions on meaning, style, and technique.

**Composition (作文)**: A paper testing writing skills through narrative, expository, argumentative, or practical writing tasks.

**Integrated Learning Module (綜合學習)**: A component connecting different language skills through real-world contexts and tasks.

**Oral Communication (口語溝通)**: A component assessing speaking skills including group discussion and individual presentation.

**Poetry Appreciation (詩歌欣賞)**: Understanding and analyzing classical and modern Chinese poetry, including imagery, themes, and techniques.

**Practical Writing (實用文)**: Writing for practical purposes such as letters, reports, and proposals following specific formats.

## English Language

**Cloze Test**: A passage with blanks where candidates select appropriate words from options or supply their own.

**Integrated Skills**: A paper combining listening, reading, and writing skills in an integrated task.

**Listening and Integrated Skills**: A paper testing comprehension of spoken English combined with reading and writing tasks.

**Reading (閱讀理解)**: A paper testing comprehension of English texts through various question types.

**Speaking**: An assessment of oral communication including group interaction and individual presentation.

**Writing (寫作)**: A paper testing writing skills in various genres and formats.

## Mathematics

**Algebra (代數)**: A branch dealing with symbols and rules for manipulating them. Includes equations, inequalities, and functions.

**Calculus (微積分)**: The study of rates of change (differentiation) and accumulation (integration).

**Circular Measure (弧度制)**: Measuring angles in radians. π radians = 180°. Arc length = rθ; sector area = ½r²θ.

**Differentiation (微分)**: Finding the derivative of a function, representing the rate of change or slope of the tangent.

**Equation (方程)**: A mathematical statement asserting the equality of two expressions. Types: linear, quadratic, polynomial.

**Function (函數)**: A relation where each input has exactly one output. Notation: f(x).

**Geometric Sequence (等比數列)**: A sequence where each term is found by multiplying the previous term by a constant ratio.

**Integration (積分)**: Finding the integral of a function, representing the area under the curve or accumulation.

**Logarithm (對數)**: The inverse of exponentiation. log_b(x) = n means bⁿ = x.

**Matrices (矩陣)**: Rectangular arrays of numbers used in linear algebra. Operations include addition, multiplication, and inversion.

**Number System (數系)**: Classification of numbers: natural, integer, rational, irrational, real, complex.

**Probability (機率)**: The measure of the likelihood of an event occurring. Ranges from 0 (impossible) to 1 (certain).

**Quadratic Equation (二次方程)**: An equation of the form ax² + bx + c = 0. Solved using factoring, completing the square, or the quadratic formula.

**Sequence (數列)**: An ordered list of numbers following a pattern. Types: arithmetic, geometric, recursive.

**Statistics (統計學)**: The study of collecting, analyzing, interpreting, and presenting data.

**Trigonometry (三角學)**: The study of relationships between angles and sides of triangles. Functions: sin, cos, tan.

## Liberal Studies

**Alternative Perspective (另類觀點)**: Different viewpoints or interpretations of an issue, essential for balanced analysis.

**Analysis (分析)**: Breaking down an issue into components to understand causes, effects, and relationships.

**China Studies (中國研究)**: A module examining China's development in areas such as economy, society, and politics.

**Contemporary Issues (當代議題)**: Current topics of social, economic, political, or environmental significance.

**Critical Thinking (批判性思考)**: Evaluating arguments, evidence, and perspectives to form reasoned judgments.

**Globalization (全球化)**: The increasing interconnectedness of economies, cultures, and societies worldwide.

**Identity and Belonging (身份與認同)**: Understanding personal and collective identity in social and cultural contexts.

**Integrated Theme (綜合主題)**: A module combining multiple perspectives on broad themes like personal development and contemporary Hong Kong.

**Issue Enquiry (議題探究)**: Investigating issues from multiple perspectives, evaluating evidence, and forming conclusions.

**Multiple Perspectives (多角度思考)**: Considering various viewpoints and dimensions of an issue for comprehensive understanding.

**Sustainability (可持續發展)**: Meeting present needs without compromising future generations' ability to meet their own needs.

**Value Judgment (價值判斷)**: Evaluating situations based on moral, ethical, or social values.

## Physics

**Acceleration (加速度)**: The rate of change of velocity. a = Δv/Δt. Unit: m/s².

**Atom (原子)**: The basic unit of matter, consisting of a nucleus (protons and neutrons) and electrons.

**Electric Circuit (電路)**: A closed path through which electric current flows. Components: source, conductors, load, switch.

**Electromagnetic Spectrum (電磁波譜)**: The range of all electromagnetic radiation, from radio waves to gamma rays.

**Energy (能量)**: The capacity to do work. Unit: joule (J). Forms: kinetic, potential, thermal, chemical.

**Force (力)**: A push or pull acting on an object. Unit: newton (N). F = ma.

**Magnetism (磁學)**: The study of magnetic fields and their effects on materials and moving charges.

**Wave (波)**: A disturbance that transfers energy without transferring matter. Types: transverse, longitudinal.

## Chemistry

**Acid (酸)**: A substance that donates H⁺ ions in aqueous solution. pH < 7.

**Alkali (鹼)**: A base that dissolves in water to form an alkaline solution. pH > 7.

**Atomic Structure (原子結構)**: The arrangement of subatomic particles (protons, neutrons, electrons) in an atom.

**Bonding (化學鍵)**: The forces holding atoms together in compounds. Types: ionic, covalent, metallic.

**Chemical Equation (化學方程式)**: A symbolic representation of a chemical reaction showing reactants and products.

**Electrolysis (電解)**: Using electric current to drive a non-spontaneous chemical reaction.

**Periodic Table (元素週期表)**: A tabular arrangement of elements by atomic number and electron configuration.

**Reaction Rate (反應速率)**: The speed at which a chemical reaction occurs. Affected by concentration, temperature, and catalysts.

## Biology

**Cell (細胞)**: The basic structural and functional unit of living organisms.

**DNA (脫氧核糖核酸)**: Deoxyribonucleic acid, the molecule carrying genetic information.

**Ecology (生態學)**: The study of interactions between organisms and their environment.

**Evolution (演化)**: The process of change in populations over generations through natural selection.

**Genetics (遺傳學)**: The study of heredity and variation in organisms.

**Homeostasis (恆態)**: The maintenance of a stable internal environment despite external changes.

**Photosynthesis (光合作用)**: The process by which plants convert light energy into chemical energy. 6CO₂ + 6H₂O → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂.

**Reproduction (繁殖)**: The process by which organisms produce offspring. Types: sexual, asexual.

## Economics

**Demand (需求)**: The quantity of a good that consumers are willing and able to buy at various prices.

**Elasticity (彈性)**: The responsiveness of quantity demanded or supplied to changes in price or income.

**GDP (國內生產總值)**: Gross Domestic Product, the total value of goods and services produced in an economy.

**Inflation (通貨膨脹)**: A sustained increase in the general price level of goods and services.

**Market (市場)**: A mechanism where buyers and sellers interact to determine prices and exchange goods and services.

**Opportunity Cost (機會成本)**: The value of the next best alternative foregone when making a choice.

**Supply (供給)**: The quantity of a good that producers are willing and able to sell at various prices.

**Unemployment (失業)**: The state of being without work while actively seeking employment.
