## Diagnostic Test: Cold War

Answer each question. Check your answers against the key at the end.

## Questions

**1.** Which policy, articulated by George Kennan in 1947, became the foundation of American Cold War strategy?
(A) Appeasement
(B) Containment
(C) Détente
(D) Isolationism

**2.** The Truman Doctrine (1947) committed the United States to:
(A) Maintaining neutrality in European affairs
(B) Supporting nations resisting communist expansion
(C) Establishing a military alliance with the Soviet Union
(D) Withdrawing from the United Nations

**3.** Which event marked the beginning of the Korean War?
(A) The fall of the Berlin Wall
(B) North Korea's invasion of South Korea
(C) The Chinese Communist Revolution
(D) The Soviet atomic bomb test

**4.** The Vietnam War is primarily characterised as:
(A) A direct superpower conflict between the United States and the Soviet Union
(B) A proxy war involving Cold War ideological and strategic competition
(C) A purely civil war with no external involvement
(D) A conflict confined to Southeast Asia without global implications

**5.** The Sino-Soviet split refers to:
(A) The division of Germany into East and West
(B) The deterioration of relations between the People's Republic of China and the Soviet Union
(C) The separation of North and South Korea
(D) The break between the United States and its NATO allies

**6.** The Cuban Missile Crisis (1962) was resolved through:
(A) Military invasion of Cuba by United States forces
(B) Diplomatic negotiation and Soviet withdrawal of missiles
(C) United Nations military intervention
(D) Nuclear exchange between the superpowers

**7.** Which agreement formally ended the Cold War era of arms limitation negotiations?
(A) The Camp David Accords
(B) The INF Treaty (1987)
(C) The Treaty of Versailles
(D) The Atlantic Charter

**8.** The fall of the Berlin Wall in 1989 symbolised:
(A) The beginning of the Cold War
(B) The reunification of Germany and the collapse of communist regimes in Eastern Europe
(C) The dissolution of NATO
(D) The start of the Korean War

**9.** Which factor is most commonly cited as contributing to the economic decline of the Soviet Union?
(A) Excessive foreign aid to Western Europe
(B) Centralised economic planning inefficiencies and military overspending
(C) Rapid industrialisation and technological innovation
(D) Membership of the European Economic Community

**10.** The Non-Aligned Movement during the Cold War represented:
(A) Nations firmly allied with the United States
(B) States that sought to remain independent from both superpower blocs
(C) Countries that supported the Soviet Union economically
(D) European nations withdrawing from NATO

## Intuition

**A tense standoff:** The Cold War was like two rivals who never fought directly but competed through proxies, arms races, and propaganda — the world held hostage by nuclear tension.

**Why it matters:** Cold War decisions shaped modern geopolitics — from NATO to the Middle East, from space exploration to surveillance states.

**The key insight:** Containment wasn't about defeating the enemy — it was about preventing their influence from spreading, a strategy that defined decades of foreign policy.

## Common Mistakes

**Assuming the Cold War was only between the US and USSR:** Many countries were involved as allies, proxies, or non-aligned states. The Sino-Soviet split shows the Communist bloc wasn't monolithic.

**Confusing the Truman Doctrine with the Marshall Plan:** The Truman Doctrine (1947) was about military/political containment. The Marshall Plan (1948) was about economic reconstruction of Europe. They're related but different policies.

**Mixing up détente with the end of the Cold War:** Détente (1970s) was a period of reduced tensions, not the end. The Cold War ended in 1989-1991 with the fall of the Berlin Wall and Soviet collapse.
