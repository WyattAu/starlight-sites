## Intuition

**The DSE is about demonstrating mastery of a broad curriculum:** Unlike A-Levels which specialise, the DSE requires competence across languages, sciences, humanities, and mathematics. This breadth tests your ability to learn diverse subjects and apply different thinking modes — logical (maths), analytical (sciences), creative (arts), and linguistic (languages).

**Why it matters:** DSE results determine university admissions in Hong Kong, but more importantly, the skills you develop — time management, exam technique, and subject mastery — prepare you for university and professional life. The DSE's rigorous marking standards mean that achieving high scores demonstrates genuine understanding.

**The key insight:** The DSE rewards exam technique as much as knowledge. Understanding the marking scheme, practising past papers under timed conditions, and knowing which topics carry the most marks can significantly improve your score. Worked examples show you exactly how examiners expect answers to be structured.

## How to Use These Notes

1. **Start with the fundamentals** -- build a solid foundation before moving to advanced topics
2. **Work through examples** -- follow along with the worked examples to build intuition
3. **Test yourself** -- use the practice problems and flashcards to check your understanding
4. **Review regularly** -- spaced repetition helps retain what you have learned

## Common Mistakes

**Trying to memorise everything instead of understanding concepts:** DSE exams test understanding, not rote memorisation. Focus on why things work, not just what they are.

**Skipping practice questions:** Reading notes is passive. Doing practice questions is active learning. Always attempt questions before checking answers.

**Not reviewing mistakes:** When you get a question wrong, don't just check the answer — understand WHY you got it wrong and review that concept.
