### Scalars and Vectors

### Practice Problem Tips

- **Read the direction carefully**: many questions have negative signs embedded in the setup. A ball thrown upward has u > 0 and a = -9.81 m/s².
- **Draw a diagram**: for multi-stage problems (e.g., a car accelerating then braking), sketch a v-t graph to visualise the motion before solving.
- **Check units**: DSE questions sometimes mix km/h with m/s. Convert first using 1 m/s = 3.6 km/h.

### Approach Strategy

- Identify which SUVAT variables are known and which is unknown.
- If time is not involved, use v² = u² + 2as.
- For projectile problems, split into horizontal (constant velocity) and vertical (constant acceleration) components.

## Intuition

Think of each practice question as a mini-story about an object moving. First, understand the story: what is moving, what forces act on it, and what is being asked. Then translate the story into equations.

## Common Mistakes

- **Forgetting to square velocity**: in v² = u² + 2as, students often write v = u + 2as or forget the square entirely.
- **Mixing up initial and final velocity**: u is initial (at t = 0), v is final (at time t). Swapping them gives wrong answers.
- **Not converting units**: if displacement is in metres, time in seconds, then velocity must be in m/s. Mixing km/h with m/s leads to incorrect magnitudes.
