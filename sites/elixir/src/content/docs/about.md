## About Wyatt's Notes — Elixir

Wyatt's Notes delivers comprehensive study materials for Elixir programming. These notes cover Elixir's concurrency model, Phoenix framework, OTP principles, and functional programming patterns, building the skills needed for scalable, fault-tolerant applications.

### Why Trust These Resources?

These notes are created by **Wyatt**, an educator and content developer with expertise in Elixir and distributed systems. Content follows a structured progression from language fundamentals through OTP to practical Phoenix web development.

Materials emphasise Elixir's actor-based concurrency, fault tolerance through supervision trees, and the functional programming patterns that make Elixir expressive. Every concept includes practical examples and explains the distributed systems principles behind Elixir's design.

### What You'll Find

- **Language fundamentals** covering syntax, pattern matching, and pipes
- **Concurrency** with processes, GenServers, and OTP
- **Phoenix framework** covering channels, live views, and Ecto
- **Fault tolerance** with supervisors and the let-it-crash philosophy
- **Distribution** across nodes and clusters

### Credentials

Wyatt develops resources across Elixir, distributed systems, and programming, providing the infrastructure perspective that enhances Elixir materials. Content is informed by Elixir specification, OTP documentation, and production system analysis.
