## About Wyatt's Notes — Gaokao

Wyatt's Notes offers study materials for the Gaokao (高考), China's national college entrance examination. The Gaokao is one of the world's most competitive exams, taken by over 10 million students annually and determining university admissions across all Chinese provinces.

### Why Trust These Resources?

These notes are created by **Wyatt**, an educator and content developer with knowledge of the Chinese national curriculum. Content covers the core subjects tested on the Gaokao, structured according to the Ministry of Education's curriculum standards.

Materials emphasise the problem-solving techniques and mathematical rigour that Gaokao examinations demand. Content is informed by analysis of past papers, provincial variations, and the exam's emphasis on both foundational knowledge and higher-order thinking.

### What You'll Find

- **Subject notes** covering core Gaokao subjects
- **Problem-solving strategies** aligned to Gaokao question formats
- **Practice questions** modelled on actual exam difficulty
- **Concept reviews** organised for efficient revision

### Credentials

Wyatt develops resources across multiple national and international qualification systems, providing a comparative perspective that enhances Gaokao content accuracy. Materials are informed by curriculum specifications, past paper analysis, and understanding of provincial exam variations.
