# Reading

高考 english 学习笔记 - Reading

## 核心概念

### 阅读理解题型

**主旨大意题：** 考查对文章主旨和中心思想的理解。

常见提问方式：
- What is the main idea of the passage?
- What is the best title for the passage?
- The passage is mainly about...

**细节理解题：** 考查对文中具体信息的理解和记忆。

常见提问方式：
- According to the passage...
- Which of the following is TRUE?
- What does the author say about...?

**推理判断题：** 考查根据文中信息进行推理判断的能力。

常见提问方式：
- It can be inferred from the passage that...
- The author implies that...
- What will the author most likely discuss next?

**词义猜测题：** 考查根据上下文推测词义的能力。

常见提问方式：
- The underlined word "..." probably means...
- What does "..." refer to in paragraph X?

### 阅读策略

**略读（Skimming）：** 快速浏览文章，抓住主旨大意。

**扫读（Scanning）：** 带着问题快速查找特定信息。

**精读（Intensive Reading）：** 仔细阅读，理解细节和深层含义。

### 常见文章结构

- 总分总结构
- 时间顺序结构
- 因果关系结构
- 对比对照结构
- 问题解决结构

## 典型例题

### 例题1：主旨大意题

**题目：** Read the following passage and answer the question.

"In recent years, artificial intelligence has made remarkable progress. From self-driving cars to virtual assistants, AI technology is becoming increasingly integrated into our daily lives. However, this rapid development also raises concerns about job displacement, privacy, and ethical issues. Experts argue that while AI brings tremendous benefits, society must carefully consider its potential drawbacks and develop appropriate regulations."

What is the main idea of the passage?

A. AI technology has no drawbacks.
B. AI development brings both benefits and concerns.
C. Self-driving cars are the most important AI application.
D. Experts disagree about the future of AI.

**解答：**

步骤1：通读全文，把握主旨

步骤2：文章先讲AI的进步，再讲带来的担忧，最后讲需要监管

步骤3：主旨是AI发展既带来好处也带来问题

步骤4：排除A（太绝对）、C（细节不是主旨）、D（文中未提及分歧）

**答案：** B

### 例题2：细节理解题

**题目：** According to the passage, which of the following is TRUE about AI?

A. AI has completely replaced human workers.
B. AI raises concerns about privacy and ethics.
C. AI has no impact on daily life.
D. Experts believe AI should be banned.

**解答：**

步骤1：回到原文查找相关信息

步骤2：原文提到"raises concerns about job displacement, privacy, and ethical issues"

步骤3：B项与原文一致

步骤4：A项"completely replaced"太绝对，C项与原文矛盾，D项"banned"文中未提及

**答案：** B

### 例题3：推理判断题

**题目：** It can be inferred from the passage that the author's attitude towards AI is ______.

A. completely negative
B. cautiously optimistic
C. strongly supportive
D. totally indifferent

**解答：**

步骤1：分析作者态度——既提到benefits也提到drawbacks

步骤2：作者没有完全否定AI，也没有完全肯定

步骤3："cautiously optimistic"（谨慎乐观）最符合

步骤4：排除A（太消极）、C（太积极）、D（漠不关心）

**答案：** B

## 考试技巧

1. 主旨大意题要关注首尾段和每段首句
2. 细节题要准确定位原文，注意同义替换
3. 推理题不能过度推断，要基于文中信息
4. 词义猜测题要利用上下文语境和构词法
5. 阅读理解一般每篇用时6-8分钟

## 练习题

1. 阅读一篇关于环境保护的短文，找出主旨大意
2. 根据上下文猜测划线单词"ubiquitous"的含义
3. 练习略读技巧：用2分钟略读一篇500词的文章并回答主旨题
