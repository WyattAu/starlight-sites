# Writing

高考 english 学习笔记 - Writing

## 核心概念

### 应用文写作

**书信类：**
- 感谢信（Thank-you letter）
- 道歉信（Apology letter）
- 申请信（Application letter）
- 建议信（Suggestion letter）
- 邀请信（Invitation letter）
- 通知（Notice）

**书信格式：**
```
Dear ...,

  [开头] 说明写信目的
  [正文] 详细内容
  [结尾] 表达期望或感谢

Yours sincerely/faithfully,
[姓名]
```

### 读后续写

**续写要求：**
- 与所给段落开头语衔接
- 内容合理，情节完整
- 使用5个以上短文中标有下划线的关键词语
- 词数150左右

**续写技巧：**
- 推理合理，情节连贯
- 保持原文的写作风格
- 适当使用心理描写和环境描写
- 结尾要积极向上

### 常用句型

**开头句型：**
- I am writing to...
- I am glad/happy to...
- I would like to express my...

**过渡句型：**
- First of all / Moreover / Furthermore
- In addition / Besides / What's more
- On the other hand / However

**结尾句型：**
- I am looking forward to hearing from you.
- Thank you for your time and consideration.
- I would appreciate it if you could...

## 典型例题

### 例题1：建议信

**题目：** 假如你是李华，你的英国朋友Tom来信说他学习压力大，请给他写一封建议信。

**解答：**

```
Dear Tom,

  I am sorry to hear that you are under great pressure from your studies.
Here are some suggestions that I hope will be helpful to you.

  First of all, it is important to make a study plan. You can divide your
tasks into small parts and complete them one by one. This will make your
study more efficient. Moreover, doing exercise regularly can help you relax
and reduce stress. Even a short walk can make a big difference. In addition,
talking to your friends or family about your feelings can also help you feel
better.

  I hope these suggestions will be useful to you. Remember, difficulties are
temporary, and you will overcome them with patience and effort.

Yours sincerely,
Li Hua
```

**答案：** 见范文

### 例题2：读后续写

**题目：** 阅读下面短文，根据所给情节进行续写。

"Tom was a shy boy who always sat alone in the classroom. One day, a new student named Jerry came to his class. Jerry was outgoing and friendly. He noticed Tom sitting alone and decided to talk to him."

**解答：**

续写示范：

"Jerry walked over to Tom and said with a warm smile, 'Hi, I'm Jerry. Do you mind if I sit here?' Tom was surprised but nodded quietly. Jerry started to talk about his hobbies and asked Tom about his. Gradually, Tom began to open up and shared his love for reading.

  From that day on, Jerry and Tom became good friends. Jerry introduced Tom to other classmates, and Tom slowly became more confident. He learned that sometimes all it takes is a small step to make a big change in life."

**答案：** 见续写示范

### 例题3：通知写作

**题目：** 你校将举办英语演讲比赛，请以学生会的名义写一则通知。

**解答：**

```
NOTICE

  The English Speech Contest will be held in the school auditorium on
Saturday, October 15th, 2026. The contest will start at 2:00 PM.

  All students are welcome to participate. The topic of the speech is
"My Dream". Each contestant will have 3 to 5 minutes to deliver their
speech. A panel of judges will select the top three winners, who will
receive prizes and certificates.

  Those who wish to participate should sign up at the Student Union
office before October 10th. For more information, please contact the
Student Union.

Student Union
October 1st, 2026
```

**答案：** 见通知范文

## 考试技巧

1. 书信格式要规范，称呼和落款要正确
2. 应用文要注意语言得体，根据对象选择正式或非正式语气
3. 读后续写要仔细阅读原文，保持人物性格和情节发展的一致性
4. 注意使用连接词使文章逻辑清晰
5. 写作时间一般控制在25-30分钟
