## Intuition

Gaokao Mathematics tests a broad range of topics from set theory and functions through calculus, analytical geometry, and probability. Functions are the central concept — understanding domain, range, monotonicity, and symmetry is essential. Derivatives measure rates of change and find extrema, while integrals accumulate areas and volumes. Analytical geometry connects algebraic equations to geometric shapes — lines, circles, ellipses, and hyperbolas.

## Common Pitfalls

- **Derivative sign errors**: Forgetting the chain rule when differentiating composite functions — d/dx[sin(2x)] = 2cos(2x), not cos(2x). Always multiply by the derivative of the inner function.
- **Ellipse formula confusion**: The standard form x²/a² + y²/b² = 1 has a > b for a horizontal major axis — mixing up a and b leads to wrong focal distances and eccentricity calculations.
- **Probability counting errors**: Forgetting to account for order in permutations vs combinations — P(n,k) = n!/(n-k)! counts ordered arrangements, while C(n,k) = n!/(k!(n-k)!) counts unordered selections.
