## Intuition

Gaokao Mathematics tests a broad range of topics from set theory and functions through calculus, analytical geometry, and probability. Functions are the central concept — understanding domain, range, monotonicity, and symmetry is essential. Derivatives measure rates of change and find extrema, while integrals accumulate areas and volumes. Analytical geometry connects algebraic equations to geometric shapes — lines, circles, ellipses, and hyperbolas.

## How to Use These Flashcards

**First pass:** Go through all 20 cards without rating — just flip and read. This familiarises you with the content and identifies weak areas.

**Rating system:** 1 = could not recall, 2 = recalled with difficulty, 3 = recalled correctly, 4 = instant recall. The spaced repetition algorithm shows cards rated 1-2 more frequently.

**Speed practice:** Gaokao is time-pressured. After mastering the content, time yourself flipping through cards to build speed.

**Common Pitfalls**

- **Derivative sign errors**: Forgetting the chain rule when differentiating composite functions — d/dx[sin(2x)] = 2cos(2x), not cos(2x). Always multiply by the derivative of the inner function.
- **Ellipse formula confusion**: The standard form x²/a² + y²/b² = 1 has a > b for a horizontal major axis — mixing up a and b leads to wrong focal distances and eccentricity calculations.
- **Probability counting errors**: Forgetting to account for order in permutations vs combinations — P(n,k) = n!/(n-k)! counts ordered arrangements, while C(n,k) = n!/(k!(n-k)!) counts unordered selections.
- **Trigonometric identity misuse**: Applying sum-to-product or double-angle formulas incorrectly — always verify the formula applies to the angles you have, not the ones you want.
- **Sequence convergence**: Assuming all geometric sequences converge — |r| must be less than 1 for the sum to converge. Forgetting this condition leads to wrong answers for infinite series.

## Study Tips

- **Master the unit circle:** Gaokao trigonometry frequently tests angle relationships. Drawing the unit circle and marking key angles (30°, 45°, 60°, 90°) helps recall exact values quickly.
- **Practise coordinate geometry with real diagrams:** For ellipse and hyperbola problems, sketch the curve and mark the foci, vertices, and asymptotes. This visual check catches formula errors.
- **Time yourself on past papers:** Gaokao mathematics papers are time-pressured. Practise completing problems within 2-3 minutes for easy questions and 5-8 minutes for difficult ones.
- **Review marking schemes:** Official Gaokao marking schemes reward specific steps and methods. Study them to understand what examiners look for — partial marks are available for correct working even with wrong answers.
- **Create formula flashcards:** Key formulae (quadratic formula, trigonometric identities, derivatives, integrals) should be recallable instantly. Use spaced repetition to maintain fluency.
