## Gaokao Structure

**Gaokao (高考)**: The National College Entrance Examination of China, the most important examination for university admissions.

**National College Entrance Examination**: The formal English name for the Gaokao.

**Province**: Each Chinese province administers its own Gaokao with slightly different formats and score thresholds.

**Liberal Arts Track (文科)**: Focuses on Politics, History, and Geography alongside Chinese, Mathematics, and English.

**Science Track (理科)**: Focuses on Physics, Chemistry, and Biology alongside Chinese, Mathematics, and English.

**New Reform (新高考)**: The reformed Gaokao system allowing student choice of subjects, adopted by provinces like Shanghai and Zhejiang.

**3+3 Model**: Three compulsory subjects (Chinese, Mathematics, English) plus three chosen subjects.

**3+1+2 Model**: Three compulsory subjects, one required from Physics/Biology, and two chosen from remaining subjects.

**985 University**: The top 39 elite universities in China, analogous to Ivy League institutions.

**211 University**: A broader group of approximately 100 universities designated as "Project 211" for development.

**Double First-Class**: China's current initiative to develop world-class universities and disciplines.

## Gaokao Scoring

**Total Score**: 750 in most provinces (660 in Shanghai).

**Chinese (语文)**: 150 points, 150 minutes.

**Mathematics (数学)**: 150 points, 120 minutes.

**English (英语)**: 150 points, 120 minutes.

**Comprehensive Liberal Arts**: 300 points, 150 minutes — covering Politics, History, Geography.

**Comprehensive Science**: 300 points, 150 minutes — covering Physics, Chemistry, Biology.

**Multiple-Choice Questions**: Select one correct answer from four options, typically 5 points each.

**Fill-in-the-Blank Questions**: Provide a specific answer, typically 5 points each.

**Free-Response Questions**: Require showing complete working and logical reasoning, typically 10–15 points each.

**Essay (作文)**: 60 points in Chinese, requiring 800+ characters on a given prompt.

**Provincial Score Thresholds**: Minimum scores for university entry vary by province, university, and year.

## Mathematics Topics

**Sets and Logic**: Set operations, logical connectives, and quantifiers.

**Functions**: Domain, range, monotonicity, parity, and composition.

**Exponential and Logarithmic Functions**: Properties, equations, and inequalities.

**Trigonometric Functions**: Identities, graphs, and applications.

**Sequences and Series**: Arithmetic and geometric sequences, summation formulas.

**Inequalities**: AM-GM inequality, Cauchy-Schwarz inequality, and methods for solving.

**Plane Analytic Geometry**: Lines, circles, ellipses, hyperbolas, and parabolas.

**Solid Geometry**: Volumes, surface areas, and spatial relationships.

**Probability and Statistics**: Classical probability, conditional probability, and statistical measures.

**Derivatives and Applications**: Differentiation rules, optimization, and curve sketching.

## Physics Topics

**Kinematics**: Motion in one and two dimensions, projectile motion, relative motion.

**Newton's Laws**: Force analysis, friction, connected systems.

**Work, Energy, and Power**: Kinetic energy theorem, potential energy, conservation of energy.

**Momentum**: Impulse, conservation of momentum, collisions.

**Circular Motion and Gravitation**: Centripetal force, satellite motion, Kepler's laws.

**Oscillations and Waves**: Simple harmonic motion, wave properties, superposition.

**Electrostatics**: Coulomb's law, electric fields, electric potential.

**Circuits**: Ohm's law, Kirchhoff's laws, AC circuits.

**Magnetism**: Magnetic fields, Lorentz force, electromagnetic induction.

**Modern Physics**: Photoelectric effect, atomic models, nuclear physics.

## Chemistry Topics

**Stoichiometry**: Mole concept, limiting reagents, yield calculations.

**Chemical Equilibrium**: Le Chatelier's principle, equilibrium constants, reaction quotients.

**Acids and Bases**: pH calculations, buffer solutions, titration.

**Thermochemistry**: Enthalpy, Hess's law, bond energies.

**Electrochemistry**: Galvanic cells, electrolysis, Nernst equation.

**Inorganic Chemistry**: Periodic trends, coordination compounds, qualitative analysis.

**Organic Chemistry**: Functional groups, reaction mechanisms, nomenclature.

**Chemical Experiments**: Laboratory procedures, apparatus, and safety.

**Ionic Equations**: Equations showing only the species that participate in a reaction.

**Redox Reactions**: Oxidation-reduction reactions involving electron transfer.

## English Topics

**Reading Comprehension**: Understanding passages and answering inference, detail, and vocabulary questions.

**Cloze (完形填空)**: Choosing correct words/phrases to complete a passage based on context.

**Grammar and Usage**: Tenses, voice, subject-verb agreement, pronoun reference, sentence structure.

**Writing (书面表达)**: Composing letters, essays, or notices based on prompts.

**Listening Comprehension**: Understanding spoken English in some provinces.

**Vocabulary**: The Gaokao tests approximately 3,500+ English words.

**Translation**: Converting Chinese text to English or vice versa.

## Chinese Topics

**Modern Reading Comprehension (现代文阅读)**: Understanding and analysing literary and non-fiction texts.

**Classical Chinese (文言文阅读)**: Translating and comprehending classical Chinese passages.

**Poetry Appreciation (古代诗歌鉴赏)**: Analysing classical Chinese poetry for meaning, technique, and emotion.

**Language Use (语言文字运用)**: Idioms, sentence correction, compression, and rhetoric.

**Writing (写作)**: Composing argumentative or narrative essays (typically 800+ characters).

**Classical Chinese**: Literary Chinese used in ancient texts, distinct from modern Mandarin.

**Idioms (成语)**: Fixed four-character expressions with specific meanings.

**Rhetoric (修辞)**: Literary devices and persuasive techniques.

## Study and Exam Strategies

**Past Papers**: Previous Gaokao examinations, the most important preparation resource.

**Mock Exam (模考)**: Practice exams simulating real Gaokao conditions.

**Timed Practice**: Completing questions under real time constraints.

**Formula Sheet**: Compilation of key formulas for quick reference.

**Revision Notes**: Concise summaries of key concepts.

**Flashcards**: Study cards for memorizing facts and formulas.

**Study Plan**: Structured schedule covering all subjects systematically.

**Pomodoro Technique**: 25-minute focused work sessions with 5-minute breaks.

**Weak Area Targeting**: Focusing extra time on subjects/topics with lowest scores.

**9-8-7 Rule**: A study strategy — 9 hours sleep, 8 hours study, 7 meals (regular eating).
