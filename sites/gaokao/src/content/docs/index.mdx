## Study Strategy

The Gaokao mathematics paper tests breadth across 12 major topic areas. Allocate study time proportionally to your weak areas rather than revising topics you already know well. Use diagnostic tests to identify gaps, then focus your practice on those specific areas. Past papers from the last 5-10 years are the most valuable resource — they reveal question patterns, difficulty distributions, and marking schemes that textbooks cannot replicate.

## How to Use These Notes

1. **Diagnostic first** — Take the diagnostic test to identify your current level and weak areas
2. **Targeted study** — Focus on topics where you scored lowest
3. **Worked examples** — Study the step-by-step solutions for each topic
4. **Flashcards** — Use spaced repetition for key formulae and definitions
5. **Past papers** — Complete past papers under timed conditions for exam preparation

## Intuition

**Gaokao preparation develops deep analytical thinking:** The Chinese college entrance examination tests not just knowledge but the ability to apply concepts to novel problems under extreme time pressure.

**Why it matters:** Gaokao performance determines university placement and career trajectory for millions of students annually.

**The key insight:** Time management during the exam is a skill in itself — knowing which questions to attempt first and when to move on can significantly affect your score.

## Common Mistakes

**Focusing only on difficult problems:** Gaokao rewards breadth and accuracy. Many students spend all their time on hard problems while losing easy marks on fundamental concepts. Master the basics first — the easy and medium questions account for the majority of marks.

**Not practising under timed conditions:** Gaokao is a timed exam. Solving problems at home without time pressure does not simulate exam conditions. Always time yourself to build speed and identify which problems take too long.

**Ignoring the marking scheme:** Gaokao marking rewards specific steps and methods. Even if your final answer is correct, omitting key working steps loses marks. Study official marking schemes to understand what examiners look for.
