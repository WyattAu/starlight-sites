## About Wyatt's Notes — GCSE

Wyatt's Notes offers comprehensive study materials for the UK General Certificate of Secondary Education (GCSE). GCSEs are the standardised qualifications taken by students in England, Wales, and Northern Ireland at age 16, forming the foundation for further study and career pathways.

### Why Trust These Resources?

These notes are developed by **Wyatt**, an educator and content creator with detailed knowledge of GCSE specifications across major exam boards (AQA, OCR, Edexcel, WJEC). Content maps directly to the current curriculum requirements, covering both Foundation and Higher tier content where applicable.

Materials are designed to build solid fundamentals while preparing students for the specific assessment formats used in GCSE examinations. The emphasis on worked examples and common misconceptions reflects analysis of examiner reports and student performance data.

### What You'll Find

- **Subject notes** covering Maths, Physics, Chemistry, and other core subjects
- **Tier-specific content** for both Foundation and Higher levels
- **Exam technique** tailored to GCSE question formats and mark schemes
- **Quick revision summaries** for efficient pre-exam preparation

### Credentials

Wyatt develops resources across multiple qualification frameworks, enabling a comparative approach that strengthens content accuracy. GCSE materials are informed by exam board specifications, published mark schemes, and ongoing curriculum analysis.
