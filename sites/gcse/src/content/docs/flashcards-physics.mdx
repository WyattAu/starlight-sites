## Intuition

Physics is about **understanding how the universe works** — from the smallest particles to the largest structures. The key insight is that complex phenomena can be explained by a small number of fundamental laws and equations.

**Energy intuition:** Energy is never created or destroyed — it only changes form. A falling ball converts gravitational potential energy to kinetic energy. A lamp converts electrical energy to light and heat. The total energy in a closed system is constant (conservation of energy). This principle underlies every physics topic.

**Forces intuition:** Forces cause changes in motion. A net force accelerates an object (F = ma). Friction opposes motion. Gravity pulls objects toward each other. The key is drawing a free-body diagram — identifying every force acting on an object — to predict its motion.

**Waves intuition:** Waves transfer energy without transferring matter. A wave on a rope moves the energy along, but each point on the rope only moves up and down. The key properties are wavelength (distance between peaks), frequency (peaks per second), and amplitude (height of peaks). Speed = wavelength × frequency.

## Common Pitfalls

**Confusing mass and weight.** Mass is measured in kilograms (kg) and is the amount of matter in an object. Weight is a force measured in newtons (N) and depends on the gravitational field strength ($W = mg$). Mass is the same everywhere; weight changes with location.

**Confusing speed and velocity.** Speed is a scalar (how fast something moves). Velocity is a vector (speed in a given direction). A car going round a circle at constant speed has constant speed but changing velocity (because the direction changes).

**Forgetting that acceleration is not the same as speed.** Acceleration is the rate of change of velocity. An object can be moving very fast (high speed) but not accelerating (constant velocity). Equally, an object can be stationary (zero speed) but accelerating (e.g., a ball at the top of its trajectory).
