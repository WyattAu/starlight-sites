## Why This Matters

GCSEs are the foundation of UK education, determining eligibility for A-Levels, apprenticeships, and further study. Strong GCSE results in Mathematics, English, and Science are essential for most career paths. These notes provide comprehensive revision material to help you achieve your best results.

## Study Approach

1. **Start early** — Begin revision at least 3 months before exams
2. **Active recall** — Test yourself regularly rather than re-reading notes
3. **Past papers** — Complete past papers under timed conditions
4. **Spaced repetition** — Review material at increasing intervals to strengthen long-term retention
5. **Target weak areas** — Use diagnostic tests to identify topics that need more attention

## Common Mistakes

**Re-reading notes instead of practising questions:** Passive re-reading creates familiarity without understanding. Past paper questions under timed conditions reveal what you actually know versus what you think you know. Work through questions, then check your answers against mark schemes.

**Ignoring command words:** "Describe", "explain", "evaluate", and "compare" require different responses. Describe means state what happens; explain means say why. Misreading the command word leads to writing the wrong type of answer and losing marks even with correct knowledge.

**Not revising formulae and definitions:** GCSE exams require you to recall specific formulae and definitions from memory. Leaving these to the last minute leads to gaps. Create a formula sheet and test yourself regularly using flashcards and spaced repetition.
