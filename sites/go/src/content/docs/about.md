## About Wyatt's Notes — Go

Wyatt's Notes delivers comprehensive study materials for Go programming. These notes cover Go's concurrency model, standard library, interfaces, and systems programming, building the skills needed for cloud infrastructure, microservices, and network services.

### Why Trust These Resources?

These notes are developed by **Wyatt**, an educator and content creator with expertise in Go and distributed systems. Content follows a structured progression from language basics through concurrency patterns to production-ready service design.

Materials emphasise Go's simplicity philosophy while teaching the concurrency patterns (goroutines, channels, select) that make Go unique. Every concept includes practical examples and highlights the idioms that experienced Go developers rely on.

### What You'll Find

- **Language basics** covering types, functions, and control flow
- **Interfaces and structs** with Go's implicit interface satisfaction
- **Concurrency** with goroutines, channels, and the select statement
- **Standard library** covering HTTP, JSON, io, and testing
- **Error handling** patterns and best practices

### Credentials

Wyatt develops resources across Go, distributed systems, and programming, providing the infrastructure perspective that enhances Go materials. Content is informed by Go specification, industry best practices, and production service analysis.
