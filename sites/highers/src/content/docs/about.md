## About Wyatt's Notes — Scottish Highers

Wyatt's Notes delivers comprehensive study materials for the Scottish Highers qualification. Highers are Scotland's primary academic qualifications, typically taken in S5 (age 16-17) and forming the main entry requirement for Scottish universities. They are the Scottish equivalent of A-Levels.

### Why Trust These Resources?

These notes are developed by **Wyatt**, an educator and content creator with knowledge of the Scottish Qualifications Authority (SQA) curriculum. Content is aligned to the current SQA specifications, covering the units and assessment criteria that define each subject.

Materials are structured to address the specific demands of Higher assessments, including the balance between course work and the final examination. Content reflects the Scottish Credit and Qualifications Framework (SCQF) standards at Level 6.

### What You'll Find

- **Subject notes** covering core Higher subjects
- **Unit-by-unit breakdowns** matching SQA curriculum structure
- **Exam preparation** with practice questions and revision guides
- **Study strategies** tailored to the Higher assessment format

### Credentials

Wyatt develops resources across multiple UK and international qualification systems, providing a comparative perspective that strengthens content accuracy. Scottish Highers materials are informed by SQA specifications, past papers, and curriculum updates.
