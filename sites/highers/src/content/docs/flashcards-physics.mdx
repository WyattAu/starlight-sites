## Intuition

Physics describes how the universe works through mathematical laws. Mechanics tells you how objects move under forces (F = ma), while dynamics extends this to rotating and oscillating systems. Electricity flows through circuits governed by Ohm's law (V = IR), and waves transfer energy without transferring matter — from sound to light. Particles at the subatomic level behave nothing like everyday objects, introducing quantum behaviour and nuclear physics.

## Common Pitfalls

- **Unit inconsistencies**: Mixing SI units (metres, seconds, kilograms) with non-SI units (cm, ms, g) without converting — always convert to SI before applying formulas.
- **Free body diagrams**: Forgetting to include all forces (especially friction and weight) or drawing forces in the wrong direction — a systematic approach prevents omissions.
- **Wave equation sign errors**: Using v = fλ without checking that frequency (Hz), wavelength (m), and speed (m/s) are in compatible units — frequency in kHz or wavelength in nm must be converted first.
