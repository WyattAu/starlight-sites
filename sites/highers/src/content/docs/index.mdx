## Intuition

**Scottish Highers build the bridge between school and university:** The subjects covered — biology, chemistry, physics, maths, computer science — form the foundation for STEM careers. These are not just exam subjects but thinking frameworks that apply across disciplines.

**Why it matters:** Highers are the gateway to university admissions in Scotland and develop the analytical skills needed for scientific and technical careers.

**The key insight:** The subjects interconnect — maths provides the language for physics and chemistry, biology relies on chemistry, and computer science underpins all modern scientific research.

## Common Mistakes

**Ignoring the Scottish Qualifications Authority marking instructions:** Higher exams are marked against specific SQA instructions. Answering correctly but not in the expected format loses marks. Study past paper marking instructions to understand what examiners reward.

**Not using standard notation:** Scottish exams require standard mathematical and scientific notation. Inventing your own notation or using non-standard abbreviations may confuse examiners. Follow the notation used in your course materials.

**Confusing Higher with Advanced Higher:** Higher and Advanced Higher have different content depth and assessment styles. Advanced Higher requires more independent study and deeper analysis. Do not assume preparation for one automatically prepares you for the other.
