## About Wyatt's Notes — HSC

Wyatt's Notes provides comprehensive study materials for the Australian Higher School Certificate (HSC). The HSC is the qualification awarded to students completing secondary school in New South Wales, with ATAR (Australian Tertiary Admission Rank) scores determining university entry.

### Why Trust These Resources?

These notes are developed by **Wyatt**, an educator and content creator with knowledge of the NSW Education Standards Authority (NESA) curriculum. Content is aligned to the current HSC syllabus documents, covering both the foundational and extension courses.

Materials are structured to address the specific demands of HSC assessments, including the balance between trial exams and the final HSC examinations. Content emphasises the depth of understanding and application skills that Band 6 results require.

### What You'll Find

- **Subject notes** for major HSC subjects including Physics and Mathematics
- **Syllabus-aligned content** matching NESA specifications
- **Exam technique** for HSC-specific question formats
- **Study guides** organised for efficient ATAR preparation

### Credentials

Wyatt develops resources across multiple qualification systems, providing a comparative perspective that enhances HSC content accuracy. Materials are informed by NESA syllabus documents, past papers, and curriculum updates.
