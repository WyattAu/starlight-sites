## Intuition

HSC Physics spans the entire scale of the physical world — from kinematics (how objects move) through energy transformations to the quantum realm. Forces cause acceleration (Newton's second law), energy is always conserved but changes form (kinetic ↔ potential ↔ thermal), and waves carry information and energy across space. Electromagnetism unifies electricity and magnetism into a single force that powers civilisation.

## Common Pitfalls

- **Projectile motion components**: Forgetting that horizontal and vertical motion are independent — gravity only affects the vertical component, so horizontal velocity remains constant (ignoring air resistance).
- **Energy conservation in inelastic collisions**: Momentum is conserved in all collisions, but kinetic energy is only conserved in elastic collisions — inelastic collisions convert KE to heat/deformation.
- **Relativity length contraction vs time dilation**: Confusing which quantity changes — moving clocks run slow (time dilation), and moving objects are shorter along the direction of motion (length contraction). The stationary observer measures both effects.
