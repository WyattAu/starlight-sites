## Why This Matters

The Higher School Certificate is the primary qualification for students completing secondary education in New South Wales, Australia. Physics at this level develops analytical thinking, problem-solving skills, and a deep understanding of the physical laws governing everything from subatomic particles to planetary motion. These notes provide structured revision material aligned with the NSW Physics syllabus.
