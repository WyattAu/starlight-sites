## Why This Matters

The Higher School Certificate is the primary qualification for students completing secondary education in New South Wales, Australia. Physics at this level develops analytical thinking, problem-solving skills, and a deep understanding of the physical laws governing everything from subatomic particles to planetary motion. These notes provide structured revision material aligned with the NSW Physics syllabus.

## Common Mistakes

**Ignoring the HSC marking criteria:** HSC exams award marks for specific criteria (knowledge, understanding, application, thinking). Even correct answers lose marks if they do not demonstrate the required criteria. Study marking centre feedback to understand what examiners reward.

**Not practising with past papers under exam conditions:** HSC is time-pressured. Solving problems at home without timing does not prepare you for the real exam. Use official past papers and strict timing to build exam technique.

**Confusing HSC content with university content:** HSC courses are designed for 17-18 year olds and have specific scope. Going beyond the syllabus wastes time and may introduce concepts that are marked differently at university level.
