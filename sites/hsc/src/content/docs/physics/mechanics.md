# Mechanics

HSC physics study notes - Mechanics

## Key Concepts

### Kinematics

**Equations of motion (constant acceleration):**
$$v = u + at$$
$$s = ut + \frac{1}{2}at^2$$
$$v^2 = u^2 + 2as$$

**Average velocity:** $\bar{v} = \frac{u + v}{2}$

### Forces

**Newton's Second Law:** $F = ma$

**Weight:** $W = mg$ (where $g \approx 9.8\,\text{m/s}^2$)

**Friction:** $f = \mu N$ where $\mu$ is the coefficient of friction

### Momentum

**Linear momentum:** $p = mv$

**Impulse:** $J = F\Delta t = \Delta p$

**Conservation of momentum:** $m_1 u_1 + m_2 u_2 = m_1 v_1 + m_2 v_2$ (when no external forces)

### Energy

**Kinetic energy:** $E_k = \frac{1}{2}mv^2$

**Gravitational potential energy:** $E_p = mgh$

**Work done:** $W = Fs\cos\theta$

**Work-energy theorem:** $W_{\text{net}} = \Delta E_k$

## Worked Examples

### Example 1: Projectile Motion

**Problem:** A ball is thrown horizontally at $20\,\text{m/s}$ from a height of $45\,\text{m}$. Find the horizontal range ($g = 10\,\text{m/s}^2$).

**Solution:**

Step 1: Find time of flight using vertical motion:
$$s = \frac{1}{2}gt^2 \Rightarrow 45 = \frac{1}{2}(10)t^2 \Rightarrow t = 3\,\text{s}$$

Step 2: Find horizontal range:
$$\text{Range} = u_x \cdot t = 20 \times 3 = 60\,\text{m}$$

**Answer:** The horizontal range is $60\,\text{m}$

### Example 2: Newton's Laws

**Problem:** A $5\,\text{kg}$ block is pushed across a rough surface with a $20\,\text{N}$ horizontal force. If the coefficient of kinetic friction is $0.3$, find the acceleration ($g = 10\,\text{m/s}^2$).

**Solution:**

Step 1: Calculate the normal force: $N = mg = 5 \times 10 = 50\,\text{N}$

Step 2: Calculate friction: $f = \mu N = 0.3 \times 50 = 15\,\text{N}$

Step 3: Find net force: $F_{\text{net}} = 20 - 15 = 5\,\text{N}$

Step 4: Apply Newton's second law: $a = \frac{F_{\text{net}}}{m} = \frac{5}{5} = 1\,\text{m/s}^2$

**Answer:** The acceleration is $1\,\text{m/s}^2$

### Example 3: Conservation of Momentum

**Problem:** A $2\,\text{kg}$ ball moving at $3\,\text{m/s}$ collides with a stationary $3\,\text{kg}$ ball. After the collision, the $2\,\text{kg}$ ball moves at $1\,\text{m/s}$. Find the velocity of the $3\,\text{kg}$ ball.

**Solution:**

Step 1: Apply conservation of momentum:
$$m_1 u_1 + m_2 u_2 = m_1 v_1 + m_2 v_2$$
$$2 \times 3 + 3 \times 0 = 2 \times 1 + 3 \times v_2$$

Step 2: Solve for $v_2$:
$$6 = 2 + 3v_2 \Rightarrow v_2 = \frac{4}{3} \approx 1.33\,\text{m/s}$$

**Answer:** The velocity of the $3\,\text{kg}$ ball is $\frac{4}{3}\,\text{m/s}$

## Exam Tips

1. Always draw a free-body diagram before applying Newton's laws
2. For projectile motion, separate horizontal and vertical components
3. Momentum is conserved only when there are no external forces
4. Check units and significant figures in your final answer

## Practice Problems

1. A car accelerates from rest at $2\,\text{m/s}^2$ for $5\,\text{s}$. Find the distance covered.
2. A $10\,\text{kg}$ object is pulled up a $30°$ incline with a force of $80\,\text{N}$. Find the acceleration (frictionless).
3. Two objects of mass $4\,\text{kg}$ and $6\,\text{kg}$ collide head-on. The $4\,\text{kg}$ object was moving at $5\,\text{m/s}$ and the $6\,\text{kg}$ object at $-3\,\text{m/s}$. After the collision, the $4\,\text{kg}$ object moves at $-1\,\text{m/s}$. Find the final velocity of the $6\,\text{kg}$ object.
