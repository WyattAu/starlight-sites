## Intuition

**Mathematics is the language of patterns and relationships:** Every mathematical concept describes a pattern — from the symmetry of geometry to the rates of change in calculus. Recognising these patterns is the key to mathematical problem-solving.

**Why it matters:** Mathematical proficiency is essential for science, engineering, technology, and financial literacy.

**The key insight:** In mathematics, the method matters as much as the answer — a correct approach with an arithmetic error earns more marks than a guessed answer.

## Common Mistakes

**Confusing probability with odds:** Probability = favorable/total outcomes. Odds = favorable/unfavorable outcomes. Converting between them: P(A) = odds/(1+odds). Using the wrong formula gives incorrect probability values.

**Forgetting boundary conditions in differential equations:** When solving $y'' + y = 0$ with $y(0) = 1, y'(0) = 0$, you must apply both conditions to find the specific solution. Using only one condition leaves an arbitrary constant, giving the general solution instead.

**Misidentifying conic sections from equations:** $x^2 + y^2 = r^2$ is a circle. $x^2/a^2 + y^2/b^2 = 1$ is an ellipse. $x^2/a^2 - y^2/b^2 = 1$ is a hyperbola. The sign between the squared terms determines the conic type.
