## Intuition

**Physics connects mathematical models to physical reality:** Every physics equation represents a relationship between measurable quantities. The art of physics is applying the right model to the right situation.

**Why it matters:** Physics understanding enables technological innovation and explains natural phenomena from everyday events to cosmic processes.

**The key insight:** Units are your built-in error checker — if the units do not balance on both sides of an equation, something is wrong.

## Common Mistakes

**Confusing centripetal and centrifugal forces:** Centripetal force is real and directed toward the center of circular motion. Centrifugal force is a fictitious force perceived in a rotating reference frame. In inertial frames, only centripetal force exists.

**Using the wrong value for g in energy calculations:** Gravitational potential energy $mgh$ assumes constant $g$ near Earths surface. For satellite orbits or large height changes, use $U = -GMm/r$. Using $mgh$ for orbital mechanics gives wrong answers.

**Confusing current and voltage in circuit analysis:** Current is the flow rate of charge (amperes). Voltage is the energy per unit charge (volts). In series circuits, current is the same; voltage divides. In parallel circuits, voltage is the same; current divides.
