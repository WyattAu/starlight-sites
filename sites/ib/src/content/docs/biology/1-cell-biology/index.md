# Cell Biology

## Topics

- [Cell Biology](./1_cell-biology) - Cell theory, prokaryotic and eukaryotic cells, membrane structure, organelles, and cell division
- [Metabolism](./2_metabolism) - Enzyme kinetics, cellular respiration, photosynthesis, ATP, metabolic pathways, fermentation

## Overview

Cell biology is the study of cell structure, function, and behavior. It is a fundamental topic in IB Biology that covers:

### Cell Structure
- **Prokaryotic cells**: Bacteria, archaea
- **Eukaryotic cells**: Animal, plant, fungal cells
- **Cell organelles**: Nucleus, mitochondria, chloroplasts, endoplasmic reticulum, Golgi apparatus

### Cell Processes
- **Cell division**: Mitosis, meiosis
- **Membrane transport**: Diffusion, osmosis, active transport
- **Cell signaling**: Hormones, receptors, signal transduction

### Metabolism
- **Enzyme kinetics**: Michaelis-Menten, inhibition
- **Cellular respiration**: Glycolysis, Krebs cycle, electron transport chain
- **Photosynthesis**: Light-dependent reactions, Calvin cycle

## Key Concepts

1. **Cell Theory**: All living organisms are composed of cells, cells are the basic unit of life, and all cells arise from pre-existing cells
2. **Membrane Structure**: Phospholipid bilayer with embedded proteins (fluid mosaic model)
3. **Organelle Function**: Each organelle has a specific function in the cell
4. **Energy Transformation**: Cells convert energy from one form to another (ATP)

## Study Tips

1. **Draw diagrams** - Visualize cell structures and processes
2. **Compare and contrast** - Prokaryotic vs eukaryotic, mitosis vs meiosis
3. **Practice with flashcards** - Memorize organelle functions
4. **Work through practice problems** - Apply concepts to real scenarios
