# Diagnostic Test: Poetry Analysis

Answer each question. Check your answers against the key at the end.

## Questions

**1.** What is the primary function of enjambment in poetry?
(A) To create a formal, structured rhyme scheme
(B) To allow a sentence to continue beyond the end of a line without pause
(C) To introduce a new stanza break
(D) To establish a consistent meter throughout the poem

**2.** Which poetic device involves the attribution of human qualities to non-human entities?
(A) Metonymy
(B) Personification
(C) Hyperbole
(D) Alliteration

**3.** In IB English Paper 1, what does "unseen poetry" refer to?
(A) Poems studied in class with extensive teacher guidance
(B) Poems encountered for the first time in the examination
(C) Poems written by the student during the examination
(D) Poems published after the examination date

**4.** The term "volta" in a sonnet refers to:
(A) The rhyme scheme of the final couplet
(B) A shift in tone, argument, or perspective
(C) The syllable count of each line
(D) The use of archaic language

**5.** Which of the following best describes "juxtaposition" in poetry?
(A) The repetition of consonant sounds at the start of words
(B) The placement of contrasting elements side by side for emphasis
(C) The use of vivid sensory imagery
(D) The creation of a consistent rhythmic pattern

**6.** What is the effect of using first-person narration in a lyric poem?
(A) It creates emotional distance between the speaker and the reader
(B) It establishes a sense of personal immediacy and subjective experience
(C) It ensures the poem is biographically accurate
(D) It eliminates the possibility of an unreliable narrator

**7.** The term "tone" in poetry analysis refers to:
(A) The physical appearance of the printed text
(B) The attitude or mood conveyed by the poet's language and choices
(C) The historical period in which the poem was written
(D) The number of syllables in each line

**8.** When comparing two poems in an IB English essay, which approach is most effective?
(A) Analysing each poem in isolation without cross-reference
(B) Identifying thematic or stylistic connections while acknowledging differences
(C) Choosing one poem as superior to the other
(D) Focusing exclusively on the historical context of each poet

**9.** What is the significance of a poem's structure (e.g., quatrains, couplets, free verse)?
(A) Structure has no bearing on meaning and is purely decorative
(B) Structure can reinforce or subvert the poem's themes and emotional arc
(C) Structure only matters in formal poetry, not in contemporary verse
(D) Structure is determined exclusively by the poet's nationality

**10.** Which of the following is an example of an "extended metaphor"?
(A) A single comparison stated explicitly in one line
(B) A metaphor developed and sustained across multiple lines or stanzas
(C) A literal description of an object
(D) A comparison using "like" or "as"

## Answer Key

| Q | Answer | Explanation |
|---|--------|-------------|
| 1 | (B) | Enjambment runs a sentence over a line break without punctuation, creating momentum and urgency or blurring syntactic boundaries. |
| 2 | (B) | Personification gives human characteristics to non-human things, enabling poets to explore abstract concepts or emotional states. |
| 3 | (B) | Unseen poetry in Paper 1 requires students to analyse unfamiliar poems using skills developed through class study. |
| 4 | (B) | The volta is a rhetorical "turn" in a sonnet where the argument shifts, deepens, or reaches resolution. |
| 5 | (B) | Juxtaposition places contrasting images, ideas, or tones adjacent to one another to highlight tensions or complexities. |
| 6 | (B) | First-person narration creates immediacy and positions the reader within the speaker's subjective emotional landscape. |
| 7 | (B) | Tone is the poet's attitude as conveyed through word choice, syntax, imagery, and rhythmic patterns. |
| 8 | (B) | Effective comparison identifies meaningful parallels and distinctions, demonstrating analytical depth rather than mere summary. |
| 9 | (B) | Structure shapes meaning; formal constraints and free verse each carry interpretive significance. |
| 10 | (B) | An extended metaphor develops a single comparison across multiple lines, creating a sustained framework for exploration. |
