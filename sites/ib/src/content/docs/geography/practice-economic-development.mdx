## Intuition

When answering development questions, always use specific country examples to support your arguments. Compare countries at different development stages to show patterns. Remember that development is multidimensional -- economic indicators alone don't tell the full story. Consider historical context, colonial legacies, and global trade structures.

## Common Mistakes

- Treating GDP per capita as a perfect measure of development. Always mention its limitations (ignores inequality, informal economy, environmental costs).
- Assuming all countries follow the same development path. Different political, cultural, and geographic contexts lead to different trajectories.
- Forgetting to consider both internal and external factors in development. Domestic policies matter, but so do global trade rules, aid, and colonial history.
