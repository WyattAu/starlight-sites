## Intuition

For freshwater questions, always link physical processes to human impacts. A hydrograph question isn't just about reading the graph -- explain what causes the shape (urbanisation, deforestation, soil type). For water scarcity, distinguish between physical scarcity (arid climate) and economic scarcity (lack of infrastructure). Use case studies like the Murray-Darling Basin, Aral Sea, or Great Man-Made River.

## Common Mistakes

- Confusing surface runoff with groundwater flow. Surface runoff moves quickly over the land surface; groundwater flow moves slowly through aquifers. Both contribute to river discharge but at very different timescales.
- Forgetting that impermeable surfaces (concrete, asphalt) increase surface runoff and reduce groundwater recharge, leading to both flooding and water table depletion.
- Assuming water management is purely a technical problem. Social, political, and economic factors (water pricing, governance, corruption) are equally important.
