## Intuition

For urban environment questions, think about the trade-offs between economic growth and environmental sustainability. Urban sprawl increases car dependency and pollution but provides affordable housing. Densification reduces sprawl but can create overcrowding. Smart cities offer efficiency but raise privacy concerns. There are no perfect solutions -- only context-dependent compromises.

## Common Mistakes

- Confusing urban sprawl with suburbanisation. Sprawl is unplanned, low-density expansion at city edges. Suburbanisation is the movement of people from inner cities to suburbs -- it can be planned or unplanned.
- Assuming all informal settlements (slums) are temporary. Many have existed for decades and have developed their own social structures, economies, and governance. Upgrading is often more effective than demolition.
- Forgetting to consider both positive and negative impacts of urbanisation. Urbanisation drives economic growth, innovation, and cultural exchange, but also creates pollution, inequality, and strain on services.
