# IB Theory of Knowledge (TOK)

## Introduction to TOK

Theory of Knowledge (TOK) is a core component of the IB Diploma Programme. It explores the nature of knowledge and how we know what we claim to know.

### TOK Exhibition (33%)

- **Format**: Exhibition of 3 objects with commentary
- **Word count**: 950 words max
- **Focus**: How TOK manifests in the real world
- **Objects**: Must be from different contexts (personal, local, global)

### TOK Essay (67%)

- **Format**: Essay responding to a prescribed title
- **Word count**: 1,600 words max
- **Focus**: Knowledge and the knower, knowledge and technology, or knowledge and the arts
- **Assessment**: Based on understanding, argument quality, and use of TOK concepts

### Assessment Criteria

| Criterion | Weight | Description |
| ----------- | -------- | ------------- |
| Understanding and Knowledge | 30% | Demonstrates understanding of TOK concepts |
| Quality of Analysis | 40% | Quality of analysis and argument |
| Use of Evidence | 30% | Quality and relevance of evidence |

## Knowledge Framework

### Scope

- What is included in this area of knowledge?
- What is excluded?
- How does scope affect knowledge?

### Perspective

- Whose perspective is being presented?
- How does perspective affect knowledge?
- Can multiple perspectives coexist?

### Methodology

- How is knowledge produced?
- What methods are used?
- How do methods affect knowledge?

### Ethics

- What ethical issues are involved?
- How should ethics guide knowledge production?
- What are the consequences of knowledge?

## Exam Tips

### TOK Exhibition

1. **Choose meaningful objects** - Objects that connect to TOK concepts
2. **Write clear commentary** - Explain how objects relate to TOK
3. **Use TOK vocabulary** - Demonstrate understanding
4. **Connect to real world** - Show practical application

### TOK Essay

1. **Understand the title** - Analyze key terms
2. **Develop a clear thesis** - State your argument
3. **Use multiple perspectives** - Consider different viewpoints
4. **Provide evidence** - Use real-world examples
5. **Evaluate strengths and weaknesses** - Show critical thinking

### General Tips

1. **Read widely** - Expose yourself to different perspectives
2. **Practice TOK thinking** - Apply TOK to everyday situations
3. **Discuss with others** - Share ideas and get feedback
4. **Review past papers** - Familiarize with question types
5. **Use TOK vocabulary** - Demonstrate understanding
