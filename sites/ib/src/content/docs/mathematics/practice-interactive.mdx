## Common Mistakes

**Forgetting to apply the product rule correctly:** d/dx[fg] = f'g + fg'. Don't just differentiate each part separately and multiply.

**Mixing up the chain rule with the product rule:** Chain rule is for nested functions: d/dx[f(g(x))]. Product rule is for multiplied functions: d/dx[f(x)g(x)]. Identify which applies.

**Confusing definite with indefinite integrals:** Definite integrals give a number (area under curve). Indefinite integrals give a family of functions (+C). Don't confuse the two.
