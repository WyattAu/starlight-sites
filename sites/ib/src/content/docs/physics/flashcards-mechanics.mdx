## Intuition

Mechanics is the branch of physics that describes motion and its causes. Kinematics describes *how* things move (displacement, velocity, acceleration) without worrying about *why*. Dynamics explains *why* through Newton's laws — forces cause acceleration, and every action has an equal and opposite reaction. Energy conservation provides a powerful shortcut: instead of tracking forces through complex paths, you can compare initial and final energy states directly.

## Common Pitfalls

- **Sign errors in kinematics**: Forgetting that velocity and acceleration are vectors — if you define "up" as positive, gravity (acting down) must be negative in your equations.
- **Normal force confusion**: The normal force is not always equal to mg — on an incline, it equals mg cos θ; in accelerating elevators, it changes with the acceleration.
- **Momentum in explosions**: Forgetting that momentum is conserved in all directions independently — a system can have zero total momentum in one direction while having non-zero momentum in another.
