## Intuition

For abnormal psychology questions, always consider multiple explanations (biological, cognitive, behavioural, psychodynamic) and treatments for each disorder. IB exams reward evaluating strengths and weaknesses of each approach. Use specific research studies to support your points -- names, dates, and findings add rigour. Remember that "abnormal" is culturally defined, not an absolute category.

## Common Mistakes

- Confusing the characteristics of phobias, OCD, and depression. Each has distinct features: phobias involve specific fears and avoidance; OCD involves obsessions and compulsions; depression involves persistent low mood and loss of interest. Mixing these up is a common error.
- Assuming one explanation is sufficient. The IB expects you to evaluate multiple approaches and show how they complement or contradict each other. A complete answer considers biological, cognitive, and behavioural factors.
- Forgetting to link explanations to treatments. If you explain that OCD has a cognitive component (dysfunctional thoughts), you should mention CBT as a treatment that addresses those thoughts.
