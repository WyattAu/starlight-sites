## Intuition

For biological level questions, always consider how biological evidence (brain scans, genetics, neurotransmitters) supports or challenges a particular explanation. Remember that biological determinism (claiming biology completely determines behaviour) is a limitation -- the interactionist approach (nature + nurture) is more nuanced and better supported. Use specific examples like the amygdala in fear conditioning or serotonin in depression.

## Common Mistakes

- Confusing the functions of brain regions. The amygdala (emotion/fear), hippocampus (memory), Broca's area (speech production), and Wernicke's area (speech comprehension) have distinct roles. Mixing them up is a frequent error.
- Assuming genetic influence means genetic determinism. Having a gene for a trait doesn't guarantee you'll express it -- epigenetics shows that environmental factors can switch genes on and off.
- Forgetting that brain imaging studies show correlation, not causation. Different brain activity in a clinical group doesn't prove that brain difference causes the condition -- the relationship could be bidirectional.
