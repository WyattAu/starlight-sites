## About Wyatt's Notes — Language Tests

Wyatt's Notes provides thorough study materials for language proficiency tests. These notes cover IELTS, TOEFL, and other standardised language assessments, building the skills needed to achieve target scores for academic and immigration purposes.

### Why Trust These Resources?

These notes are created by **Wyatt**, an educator and content developer with expertise in language assessment preparation. Content follows the test formats and scoring criteria of major language proficiency examinations.

Materials emphasise the test-taking strategies and language skills that maximise scores. Every concept reflects the actual test format and the scoring rubrics that determine band scores and proficiency levels.

### What You'll Find

- **IELTS preparation** covering Academic and General Training modules
- **TOEFL preparation** with iBT format guidance
- **CEFR levels** from A1 to C2 with proficiency descriptors
- **Test strategy** for reading, writing, listening, and speaking sections
- **Practice tests** modelled on official exam formats

### Credentials

Wyatt develops resources across language testing and international qualifications, providing the assessment expertise that enhances language test materials. Content is informed by test specifications, scoring rubrics, and analysis of high-scoring responses.
