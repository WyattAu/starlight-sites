## Why This Guide Exists

Language proficiency tests are the gateway to international education, professional migration, and global career opportunities. Universities, employers, and immigration authorities worldwide require proof of English language ability, and the test you choose and the score you achieve can determine your future path.

The four major English proficiency tests each serve different purposes. IELTS is accepted by universities and immigration authorities across the UK, Australia, Canada, and New Zealand. TOEFL iBT is the standard for US and Canadian university admissions. The Duolingo English Test has emerged as a convenient, affordable alternative accepted by over 4,000 institutions. Cambridge exams (FCE, CAE, CPE) provide lifelong qualifications with no expiry date.

Beyond English, language proficiency tests exist for many languages: DELE for Spanish, DELF/DALF for French, TestDaF for German, JLPT for Japanese, and HSK for Chinese. This guide focuses primarily on English proficiency tests, with coverage of other major language exams where relevant.

This hub page maps every resource on this site, organised by test and skill area, with study plans and strategies to help you achieve the score you need.

## IELTS

The International English Language Testing System (IELTS) is jointly managed by the British Council, IDP: IELTS Australia, and Cambridge Assessment English. It is the world's most widely taken English proficiency test, with over 3.5 million tests taken annually.

### Topic Notes

- [IELTS Listening](ielts/listening) -- 4 sections, 40 questions, 30 minutes plus 10 minutes transfer time

- [IELTS Writing](../../../../gaokao/src/content/docs/chinese/writing) -- Task 1 (graph description or letter) and Task 2 (essay), 60 minutes total
- [IELTS Speaking](ielts/speaking) -- face-to-face interview in 3 parts, 11 to 14 minutes
- [IELTS Band Scores](ielts/bands) -- how the 0 to 9 band scale works and what each score means

### Practice and Review

- [Flashcards: IELTS Vocabulary](flashcards-ielts-vocabulary)

- [IELTS Speaking Prompts](ielts/speaking-prompts)
- [Mock IELTS Tests](ielts/mock)

### Key Test Focus

IELTS Academic and General Training differ in Reading and Writing. Academic Reading uses academic passages; General Training uses everyday texts. Academic Writing Task 1 requires describing graphs and charts; General Training Task 1 requires writing a letter. The Listening and Speaking tests are identical for both versions. Aim for the score your target institution requires: typically 6.0 to 7.5 for university admission.

## TOEFL iBT

The Test of English as a Foreign Language (TOEFL) iBT is administered by ETS and is the preferred English proficiency test for US and Canadian universities. The test is entirely computer-based and delivered at test centres or at home via proctoring.

### Topic Notes

- [TOEFL iBT Overview](toefl) -- test format, scoring (0 to 120), and section structure
- [TOEFL Reading](../../../../gaokao/src/content/docs/chinese/reading) -- 2 to 3 academic passages, 30 to 54 minutes, 10 questions per passage
- [TOEFL Listening](toefl/listening) -- lectures and conversations, 28 to 41 minutes, 28 to 39 questions
- [TOEFL Speaking](toefl/speaking) -- 4 integrated tasks, 17 minutes, recorded and scored by AI and human raters
- [TOEFL Writing](../../../../gaokao/src/content/docs/chinese/writing) -- integrated writing (20 minutes) and writing for an academic discussion (10 minutes)
- [TOEFL Score Guide](toefl/scores) -- section scores (0 to 30), total score (0 to 120), and institutional requirements

### Practice and Review

- [Flashcards: TOEFL Vocabulary](flashcards-toefl-vocabulary)
- [Practice Questions: TOEFL Reading](../../../../gaokao/src/content/docs/chinese/reading)
- [Practice Questions: TOEFL Writing](../../../../gaokao/src/content/docs/chinese/writing)
- [TOEFL Speaking Practice](toefl/speaking-practice)
- [Mock TOEFL Tests](toefl/mock)

### Key Test Focus

TOEFL iBT tests academic English in an integrated format: you read a passage, listen to a lecture, and then speak or write about both. This integration means you cannot prepare for each skill in isolation. The reading section tests vocabulary in academic context. The listening section requires note-taking during lectures. The speaking section tests your ability to synthesise information from reading and listening into coherent responses.

## Duolingo English Test

The Duolingo English Test (DET) has rapidly gained acceptance as a convenient, affordable alternative to IELTS and TOEFL. The test is taken online, costs significantly less than traditional tests, and provides results within 48 hours.

### Topic Notes

### Practice and Review

- [Flashcards: DET Vocabulary](flashcards-det-vocabulary)
- [Practice Questions: DET](practice-det)
- [DET Speaking Prompts](duolingo/speaking-prompts)

### Key Test Focus

The DET adapts to your ability level in real time: harder questions appear as you answer correctly. The test includes a variety of question types: read-aloud, fill-in-the-blank, dictation, speaking samples, and writing samples. The unscored interview section is sent directly to institutions, so treat it as part of your application. Preparation should focus on general English ability rather than test-specific tricks.

## Cambridge English Exams

Cambridge Assessment English offers a suite of qualifications at different levels: B1 Preliminary (PET), B2 First (FCE), C1 Advanced (CAE), and C2 Proficiency (CPE). These exams are recognised worldwide and provide lifelong qualifications with no expiry date.

### Topic Notes

- [Cambridge Exams Overview](cambridge) -- the exam suite, levels, and CEFR alignment
- [B2 First (FCE)](cambridge/fce) -- the upper-intermediate level exam and its format
- [C1 Advanced (CAE)](cambridge/cae) -- the advanced level exam, widely accepted for university admission
- [C2 Proficiency (CPE)](cambridge/cpe) -- the highest level, demonstrating near-native proficiency
- [Cambridge Scoring](cambridge/scoring) -- the A, B, C grading system and CEFR equivalence

### Practice and Review

- [Flashcards: Cambridge Vocabulary](flashcards-cambridge-vocabulary)

- [Cambridge Writing Tasks](../../../../gaokao/src/content/docs/chinese/writing)
- [Cambridge Speaking Practice](cambridge/speaking)

### Key Test Focus

Cambridge exams test all four skills (Reading, Writing, Listening, Speaking) with Use of English testing grammar and vocabulary in context. The C1 Advanced (CAE) is the most commonly required Cambridge exam for university admission. The exams include a broad range of text types and question formats. Preparation should build general English ability alongside familiarity with the specific task types.

## Test Comparison Guide

Choosing the right test depends on your target institution, timeline, and personal preferences.

| Feature | IELTS | TOEFL iBT | DET | Cambridge C1 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Format | Paper or computer | Computer only | Online (at home) | Paper or computer |
| Duration | 2 hours 45 minutes | 2 hours | 1 hour | 2 hours 30 minutes |
| Score Range | 0 to 9 (bands) | 0 to 120 | 10 to 160 | A, B, C (grade) |
| Validity | 2 years | 2 years | 2 years | Lifetime |
| Cost | $245 to $260 | $200 to $300 | $59 | $200 to $300 |
| Results | 13 calendar days | 6 to 10 days | 48 hours | 2 to 4 weeks |
| Acceptance | Global | US/Canada focused | 4,000+ institutions | Global |

### Which Test Should I Choose?

- **Targeting UK, Australia, or Canada immigration?** IELTS is the standard choice.
- **Targeting US or Canadian universities?** TOEFL iBT is widely preferred.
- **Want the fastest, most affordable option?** The Duolingo English Test is the most convenient.
- **Want a lifelong qualification?** Cambridge exams (CAE or CPE) never expire.
- **Need to show improvement over time?** Take different tests for different purposes.

## Recommended Study Plan

Preparing for a language proficiency test requires consistent practice across all four skills over 4 to 8 weeks.

### Weeks 1 to 2: Diagnostic and Foundation

- Take a full-length practice test under timed conditions
- Score your test and identify your weakest skill
- Begin daily vocabulary study: 15 to 20 new words per day
- Start listening to English podcasts or lectures for 20 minutes daily

### Weeks 3 to 4: Skill Building

- Work through reading practice daily: one passage per day
- Write one essay per week and review it against model answers
- Practise speaking for 10 minutes daily: record yourself and listen back
- Complete listening exercises focusing on your weakest question types

### Weeks 5 to 6: Test Practice

- Take one full practice test per week under real conditions
- Review every wrong answer: understand why the correct answer is right
- Focus on time management: practise pacing in each section
- Continue vocabulary and listening practice

### Weeks 7 to 8: Final Preparation

- Take your final practice test: aim for your target score or above
- Review grammar and vocabulary flashcards
- Do light practice: one timed section per day
- Rest before test day: sleep and nutrition affect performance

### Daily Routine

| Time | Activity |
| ------ | ---------- |
| Morning | Vocabulary flashcards: 15 minutes |
| Midday | Reading practice: one passage (20 minutes) |
| Afternoon | Listening practice: one lecture or podcast (20 minutes) |
| Evening | Writing or speaking practice: 20 minutes |
| Weekend | Full practice test + review |

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How should I start using this site?

Take a full-length practice test in the exam you are targeting. This tells you your current level and which skills need the most work. Then prioritise studying your weakest skill while maintaining your stronger ones.

### How long should I prepare for a language proficiency test?

Most candidates benefit from 4 to 8 weeks of consistent preparation. Studying 1 to 2 hours per day is more effective than cramming. The key is regular practice across all four skills, not marathon study sessions.

### Can I prepare for multiple tests at the same time?

It is possible but not ideal. Each test has its own format, timing, and question types. If you must prepare for two tests, focus on the core skills (vocabulary, grammar, reading, listening, speaking) that transfer between tests, and add test-specific practice for each exam.

### What is the easiest English proficiency test?

No test is universally easiest. IELTS is often considered more straightforward in format. TOEFL iBT is more academically demanding. The Duolingo English Test is shorter and more affordable. The best test for you is the one that matches your target institution's requirements.

### How do I improve my writing score quickly?

Write regularly and get feedback. Practise writing essays under timed conditions. Study the scoring criteria for your target test and ensure you address every part of the task. Read model essays and analyse their structure, vocabulary, and grammar.

### Is the Duolingo English Test accepted everywhere?

The DET is accepted by over 4,000 institutions, including many top universities. However, some institutions still require IELTS or TOEFL. Check the specific requirements of your target institution before choosing the DET.

### How many times can I take these tests?

IELTS and TOEFL can be taken as many times as you wish (IELTS has no minimum gap between sittings; TOEFL requires a 3-day gap). The DET can be taken once every 21 days. Cambridge exams are offered at set dates, typically 2 to 4 times per year.
