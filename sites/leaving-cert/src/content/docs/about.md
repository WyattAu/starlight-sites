## About Wyatt's Notes — Irish Leaving Certificate

Wyatt's Notes offers comprehensive study materials for the Irish Leaving Certificate. The Leaving Cert is the final examination of the Irish secondary school system, taken at age 17-18 and serving as the primary entry qualification for Irish and international universities.

### Why Trust These Resources?

These notes are created by **Wyatt**, an educator and content developer with knowledge of the Irish curriculum framework. Content is aligned to the National Council for Curriculum and Assessment (NCCA) specifications and the State Examinations Commission (SEC) assessment standards.

Materials are structured to address the specific demands of Leaving Cert exams, including the balance between ordinary and higher level papers. Content emphasises the analytical depth and application skills that distinguish higher grades.

### What You'll Find

- **Subject notes** covering core Leaving Cert subjects
- **Level-specific content** for Ordinary and Higher levels
- **Exam technique** aligned to SEC marking schemes
- **Revision guides** organised for efficient pre-exam study

### Credentials

Wyatt develops resources across multiple international qualification systems, providing a comparative perspective that enhances content accuracy. Leaving Cert materials are informed by SEC specifications, past papers, and curriculum updates.
