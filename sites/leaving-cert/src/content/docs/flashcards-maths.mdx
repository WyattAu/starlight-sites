## Intuition

Mathematics is the language of patterns and relationships. Algebra provides tools for solving unknowns (x in equations), calculus describes change (derivatives) and accumulation (integrals), geometry handles shapes and space, and trigonometry connects angles to side lengths in triangles. Probability quantifies uncertainty — the likelihood of events occurring — while statistics draws conclusions from data.

## Common Pitfalls

- **Minus sign distribution**: Forgetting to distribute the negative sign across all terms when removing brackets — -(3x - 2) = -3x + 2, not -3x - 2.
- **Trig identity mix-ups**: Confusing sin²θ + cos²θ = 1 with 1 + tan²θ = sec²θ — each identity applies in different contexts.
- **Simultaneous equation elimination**: Subtracting equations incorrectly — if you subtract the second from the first, you must subtract every term, including constants.
