## Intuition

**The Leaving Certificate is Ireland's gateway to higher education:** The subjects covered — biology, chemistry, physics, maths, computer science — develop analytical thinking and scientific literacy that serve you well beyond the exam.

**Why it matters:** These subjects open doors to university courses and careers in science, engineering, medicine, and technology.

**The key insight:** The exam rewards understanding and application, not just memorisation — showing you can *use* knowledge earns more marks than directly stating it.

## Common Mistakes

**Not reading the question carefully:** Leaving Cert exam questions often have multiple parts. Missing a part means losing easy marks. Read the entire question before starting, and answer all parts.

**Leaving answers in unsimplified form:** Examiners expect final answers in simplest form. Leaving sqrt(12) instead of 2*sqrt(3) may lose marks for not completing the calculation.

**Not showing working:** Even if your final answer is wrong, correct working earns partial marks. Examiners cannot award marks for invisible reasoning. Write out each step evidently.
