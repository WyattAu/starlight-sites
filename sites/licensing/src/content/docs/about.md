## About Wyatt's Notes — Licensing

Wyatt's Notes provides thorough study materials for software licensing. These notes cover open-source licences, proprietary licensing models, copyright law basics, and compliance requirements, building the skills needed to navigate the legal landscape of software development.

### Why Trust These Resources?

These notes are developed by **Wyatt**, an educator and content creator with expertise in software law and open-source compliance. Content follows a structured progression from licence types through compliance requirements to practical licensing decisions.

Materials emphasise the practical implications of licensing choices for developers and organisations. Every concept includes real-world examples and explains the obligations and restrictions that different licences impose.

### What You'll Find

- **Licence categories** covering permissive, copyleft, and proprietary models
- **Key licences** including MIT, GPL, Apache, and BSD
- **Compliance requirements** with attribution, source distribution, and modification notices
- **Patent considerations** and patent grant implications
- **Licence compatibility** and combining code under different licences

### Credentials

Wyatt develops resources across software engineering, legal compliance, and open-source practice, providing the interdisciplinary perspective that enhances licensing materials. Content is informed by licence text analysis, legal guidance, and open-source community best practices.
