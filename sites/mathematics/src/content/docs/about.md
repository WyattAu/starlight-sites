## About Wyatt's Notes — Mathematics

Wyatt's Notes provides comprehensive study materials for university-level mathematics. These notes cover linear algebra, calculus, differential equations, abstract algebra, and more, building from foundational concepts to advanced mathematical theory.

### Why Trust These Resources?

These notes are developed by **Wyatt**, an educator and content creator with thorough knowledge of mathematical pedagogy. Content follows the standard undergraduate mathematics curriculum, emphasising proofs, rigour, and the logical structure that defines mathematical thinking.

Materials balance formal definitions and theorems with intuitive explanations and worked examples. The approach reflects how mathematics is taught at leading universities, where understanding why a result holds is as important as knowing the result itself.

### What You'll Find

- **Linear algebra** covering vector spaces, matrices, eigenvalues, and inner products
- **Calculus** including single and multivariable analysis
- **Differential equations** for ordinary and partial differential equations
- **Abstract algebra** covering groups, rings, and fields
- **Proof techniques** including direct proof, contradiction, and induction

### Credentials

Wyatt develops resources across mathematics, physics, and computer science, enabling interdisciplinary connections that deepen mathematical understanding. Materials are informed by standard university curricula, research-level insights, and pedagogical best practice.
