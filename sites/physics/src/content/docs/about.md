## About Wyatt's Notes — Physics

Wyatt's Notes delivers rigorous study materials for undergraduate physics. These notes cover classical mechanics, electromagnetism, quantum mechanics, thermal physics, and optics, providing the derivation-based approach essential for physics students and researchers.

### Why Trust These Resources?

These notes are created by **Wyatt**, an educator and content developer with deep knowledge of physics pedagogy. Content follows the standard undergraduate physics curriculum, building from classical mechanics through quantum theory with mathematical rigour.

Materials emphasise derivations, first principles reasoning, and the physical intuition behind equations. Every result is connected to experimental evidence and real-world applications, reflecting the way physics is taught at leading universities.

### What You'll Find

- **Core mechanics** including Newtonian, Lagrangian, and Hamiltonian formulations
- **Electromagnetism** covering Maxwell's equations and electromagnetic radiation
- **Quantum mechanics** with wave functions, operators, and perturbation theory
- **Thermal physics** connecting statistical mechanics to thermodynamics
- **Optics** covering wave optics, interference, and diffraction

### Credentials

Wyatt develops resources across physics, mathematics, and computer science, enabling interdisciplinary connections that deepen physics understanding. Materials are informed by standard university curricula, leading textbooks, and research-level insights.
