## Common Mistakes

**Confusing Newton's third law pairs with action-reaction in different frames:** Third law pairs act on different bodies simultaneously. They are not "equal and opposite" in a non-inertial frame where fictitious forces appear.

**Forgetting that work is a scalar, not a vector:** Work W = F·d is the dot product, which can be negative. Sign errors in work lead to wrong kinetic energy changes via the work-energy theorem.

**Mixing up rotational and translational kinetic energy:** Translational: ½mv². Rotational: ½Iω². For rolling without slipping, both contribute. Using only one gives incomplete energy conservation.
