These revision notes support students preparing for a variety of international secondary-level qualifications, including GCSE, AP, Scottish Highers, Irish Leaving Certificate, CBSE, and Gaokao. Content is tailored to the specific assessment objectives and syllabus requirements of each qualification framework.

## Structure and Approach

Each qualification section is organised by subject and topic, with notes written to match the depth and style expected at the relevant level. Definitions are clearly stated, concepts are explained with diagrams or examples where appropriate, and practice questions are provided to consolidate understanding.

## Using These Notes Effectively

Begin by identifying the topics specified in your syllabus, then study the corresponding notes section in detail. Use the practice questions to test your recall and application of knowledge, and refer to your course textbook for any areas that require further clarification. Regular self-testing is one of the most effective strategies for long-term retention.
