## About Wyatt's Notes — Ruby

Wyatt's Notes offers comprehensive study materials for Ruby programming. These notes cover Ruby's object model, metaprogramming, Rails framework, and web application development, building the skills needed for rapid web development and scripting.

### Why Trust These Resources?

These notes are created by **Wyatt**, an educator and content developer with expertise in Ruby and Rails ecosystems. Content follows a structured progression from language fundamentals through metaprogramming to practical Rails development.

Materials emphasise Ruby's object-oriented everything philosophy, blocks and iterators, and the convention-over-configuration approach that defines Rails. Every concept includes practical examples and explains the Ruby way of solving problems.

### What You'll Find

- **Language fundamentals** covering types, blocks, and iterators
- **Object model** with classes, modules, and mixins
- **Metaprogramming** including method_missing, define_method, and eval
- **Rails framework** covering MVC, ActiveRecord, and RESTful design
- **Testing** with RSpec and Minitest

### Credentials

Wyatt develops resources across Ruby, web development, and programming, providing the full-stack perspective that enhances Ruby materials. Content is informed by Ruby specification, Rails guides, and production web application analysis.
