## About Wyatt's Notes — SAT

Wyatt's Notes offers thorough preparation materials for the SAT, the standardised test used for US college admissions. The SAT assesses reading, writing, and mathematics skills, with scores ranging from 400 to 1600 and used by universities worldwide for admissions decisions.

### Why Trust These Resources?

These notes are created by **Wyatt**, an educator and content developer with expertise in SAT test preparation. Content is aligned to the College Board's official test specifications, covering both the Evidence-Based Reading and Writing and the Math sections.

Materials emphasise the test-taking strategies and content knowledge that maximise scores. Content reflects analysis of official practice tests, scoring rubrics, and the digital SAT format introduced in 2024.

### What You'll Find

- **Subject-specific review** for Reading, Writing, and Mathematics
- **Test strategy guides** covering timing, question analysis, and elimination
- **Practice problems** modelled on official SAT question styles
- **Score improvement techniques** based on test pattern analysis

### Credentials

Wyatt develops resources across multiple assessment frameworks, providing a comparative perspective that strengthens SAT preparation materials. Content is informed by College Board specifications, official practice tests, and ongoing test format analysis.
