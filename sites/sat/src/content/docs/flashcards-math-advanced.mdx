## Intuition

Advanced SAT math covers the most challenging topics that distinguish top scorers — quadratics, trigonometry, coordinate geometry, exponentials, logarithms, and complex numbers. Quadratics model parabolic relationships and can be solved by factoring, completing the square, or the quadratic formula. Trigonometry connects angles to side lengths in right triangles and extends to the unit circle. Logarithms are the inverse of exponentiation, useful for solving equations where the unknown is in the exponent.

## Common Pitfalls

- **Discriminant misinterpretation**: b² - 4ac > 0 means two real roots, = 0 means one repeated root, < 0 means no real roots — confusing these leads to wrong conclusions about solutions.
- **Unit circle sign errors**: In the unit circle, cosine is the x-coordinate and sine is the y-coordinate — mixing these up produces wrong values in quadrants II, III, and IV.
- **Logarithm change of base**: Forgetting that log_b(x) = ln(x)/ln(b) — the change of base formula is essential when your calculator only has natural log.
