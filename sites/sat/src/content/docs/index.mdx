## Intuition

**SAT preparation is about developing test-taking strategies:** Beyond content knowledge, the SAT rewards time management, strategic guessing, and familiarity with question patterns. Preparation is as much about technique as knowledge.

**Why it matters:** SAT scores influence college admissions decisions and scholarship awards.

**The key insight:** Practice under timed conditions is essential — knowing the material is not enough if you cannot apply it quickly and accurately under test conditions.

## Study Tips

1. **Take a diagnostic test first**: Identify your strengths and weaknesses before studying. Focus preparation on areas where you scored lowest.
2. **Use spaced repetition**: Review flashcards daily. The spaced repetition algorithm shows you cards you struggle with more frequently.
3. **Practice under timed conditions**: The SAT is timed. Get used to working quickly and accurately.
4. **Review every mistake**: Understanding why you got a question wrong is more valuable than getting it right. Analyse your errors systematically.
5. **Use the built-in calculator**: The digital SAT allows a calculator for all math sections. Familiarise yourself with its functions.

## Common Mistakes

**Not understanding the digital SAT format:** The digital SAT is adaptive — difficulty adjusts based on your performance. Understanding the format (timing, question types, adaptive nature) is essential for preparation.

**Skipping diagnostic testing:** Before studying, take a full practice test to identify weak areas. Random studying without knowing your starting point wastes time. Focus preparation on areas where you scored lowest.

**Ignoring time management:** The SAT is timed. Spending too long on hard questions means you cannot attempt easier questions later. Flag difficult questions and return to them if time permits. Never leave questions unanswered — there is no penalty for guessing.
