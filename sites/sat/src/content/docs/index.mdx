## Common Mistakes

**Not understanding the digital SAT format:** The digital SAT is adaptive — difficulty adjusts based on your performance. Understanding the format (timing, question types, adaptive nature) is essential for preparation.

**Skipping diagnostic testing:** Before studying, take a full practice test to identify weak areas. Random studying without knowing your starting point wastes time. Focus preparation on areas where you scored lowest.

**Ignoring time management:** The SAT is timed. Spending too long on hard questions means you cannot attempt easier questions later. Flag difficult questions and return to them if time permits. Never leave questions unanswered — there is no penalty for guessing.
