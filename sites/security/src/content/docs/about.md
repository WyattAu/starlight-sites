## About Wyatt's Notes — Security

Wyatt's Notes delivers thorough study materials for cybersecurity. These notes cover network security, cryptography, vulnerability analysis, and secure development practices, building the skills needed to protect systems and data.

### Why Trust These Resources?

These notes are developed by **Wyatt**, an educator and content creator with expertise in security engineering. Content follows a structured progression from security fundamentals through offensive and defensive techniques to secure architecture design.

Materials emphasise the threat modelling and risk assessment thinking that security professionals need. Every concept includes practical examples and explains the attack vectors that security measures are designed to prevent.

### What You'll Find

- **Security fundamentals** covering CIA triad, threat modelling, and risk assessment
- **Cryptography** with symmetric, asymmetric, and hash functions
- **Network security** covering firewalls, IDS/IPS, and VPNs
- **Application security** with OWASP Top 10 and secure coding
- **Incident response** and forensics basics

### Credentials

Wyatt develops resources across security, networking, and systems administration, providing the infrastructure perspective that enhances security materials. Content is informed by industry standards (NIST, ISO 27001), security research, and production security operations.
