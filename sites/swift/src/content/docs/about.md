## About Wyatt's Notes — Swift

Wyatt's Notes offers comprehensive study materials for Swift programming. These notes cover Swift's type system, protocol-oriented design, SwiftUI, and iOS development patterns, building the skills needed for Apple platform applications.

### Why Trust These Resources?

These notes are developed by **Wyatt**, an educator and content creator with expertise in Swift and Apple platform development. Content follows a structured progression from language fundamentals through protocol-oriented design to practical SwiftUI development.

Materials emphasise Swift's safety features, value semantics, and protocol-oriented approach. Every concept includes practical examples and explains the design principles that make Swift expressive and performant on Apple platforms.

### What You'll Find

- **Language fundamentals** covering types, optionals, and control flow
- **Protocol-oriented design** with protocols, extensions, and generics
- **SwiftUI** covering views, state management, and declarative UI
- **Value semantics** with structs, copy-on-write, and immutability
- **Concurrency** with async/await and structured concurrency

### Credentials

Wyatt develops resources across Swift, mobile development, and programming, providing the Apple ecosystem perspective that enhances Swift materials. Content is informed by Swift specification, Apple development guidelines, and production app analysis.
