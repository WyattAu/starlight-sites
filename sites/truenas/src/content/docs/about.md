## About Wyatt's Notes — TrueNAS

Wyatt's Notes delivers thorough study materials for TrueNAS storage systems. These notes cover TrueNAS configuration, ZFS administration, storage pool management, and network-attached storage deployment, building the skills needed to manage enterprise and home storage solutions.

### Why Trust These Resources?

These notes are developed by **Wyatt**, an educator and content creator with expertise in storage systems and data management. Content follows a structured progression from installation through ZFS concepts to advanced storage configurations.

Materials emphasise the ZFS philosophy of data integrity and the practical administration skills that TrueNAS administrators need. Every concept includes practical examples and explains the data protection mechanisms that make TrueNAS reliable.

### What You'll Find

- **Installation and setup** covering initial configuration and network setup
- **ZFS concepts** with pools, datasets, snapshots, and replication
- **Storage management** including RAID configurations and disk management
- **Sharing protocols** covering SMB, NFS, and iSCSI
- **Backup strategies** with replication, cloud sync, and local backups

### Credentials

Wyatt develops resources across storage, Linux, and networking, providing the infrastructure perspective that enhances TrueNAS materials. Content is informed by TrueNAS documentation, ZFS best practices, and production storage administration.
