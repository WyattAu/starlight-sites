## How to Use These Notes

1. **Start with prerequisites** — each topic builds on previous material; follow the dependency chain
2. **Work through proofs** — these notes emphasise understanding *why* results hold, not just *what* they say
3. **Attempt problems before reading solutions** — active recall is more effective than passive reading
4. **Cross-reference with textbooks** — these notes supplement, not replace, standard university texts

## Study Strategy

- **Spaced repetition** — revisit material at increasing intervals to build long-term retention
- **Feynman technique** — explain concepts in plain language to identify gaps in understanding
- **Practice examinations** — work through past papers under timed conditions
- **Study groups** — discussing proofs and problem approaches deepens comprehension
- **Office hours** — engage with instructors when concepts are unclear
